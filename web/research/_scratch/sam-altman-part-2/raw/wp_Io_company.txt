'''io Products, Inc.''' is an American [[artificial intelligence]] hardware company founded in 2024 by [[Jony Ive]], Scott Cannon, Evans Hankey and Tang Tan.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |last=Gurman |first=Mark |date=May 21, 2025 |title=OpenAI to Buy AI Device Startup From Apple Veteran Jony Ive in $6.5 Billion Deal |url=https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-05-21/love-letter-late-night-ice-cream-how-charter-finally-wooed-cox |website=Bloomberg}}</ref> The company was acquired by [[OpenAI]] in May 2025 to lead its hardware product development.<ref name=":1">{{Cite news |last1=Isaac |first1=Mike |last2=Metz |first2=Cade |date=2025-05-21 |title=OpenAI Unites With Jony Ive in $6.5 Billion Deal to Create A.I. Devices |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/21/technology/openai-jony-ive-deal.html |access-date=2025-06-03 |work=The New York Times |language=en-US |issn=0362-4331}}</ref>

== History ==
io was founded in 2024 by Jony Ive, Scott Cannon, Evans Hankey and Tang Tan to develop a range of hardware products that use [[artificial general intelligence]].<ref name=":3" />

In September 2024, ''[[The New York Times]]'' reported that Ive and his design collective [[LoveFrom]] was working with OpenAI on the development of an artificial intelligence hardware device that is "less socially disruptive than the iPhone".<ref>{{Cite news |last=Mickle |first=Tripp |date=2024-09-21 |title=After Apple, Jony Ive Is Building an Empire of His Own |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2024/09/21/technology/jony-ive-apple-lovefrom.html |access-date=2025-06-03 |work=The New York Times |language=en-US |issn=0362-4331}}</ref>

In 2024, io raised $225 million from multiple investors including [[Sutter Hill Ventures]], the [[Emerson Collective]], [[SV Angel]], Maverick Ventures and [[Thrive Capital]], as well as Ive who reportedly owned an 11 per cent stake.<ref name=":3" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Liu |first=Phoebe |title=Apple Design Guru Jony Ive To Become A Billionaire Thanks To OpenAI |url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/phoebeliu/2025/05/23/-jony-ive-billionaire-with-sale-of-startup-io-openai/ |access-date=2025-06-03 |website=Forbes |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Pringle |first=Eleanor |title=Laurene Powell Jobs has the inside track on friend Jony Ive's collaboration with OpenAI—but still wants to see Apple succeed |url=https://fortune.com/2025/06/02/laurene-powell-jobs-jony-ives-openai-apple/ |access-date=2025-06-03 |website=Fortune |language=en}}</ref> It was reported that OpenAI founder [[Sam Altman]] did not have equity in the company.<ref name=":3" /> The company also received an investment that year from OpenAI’s Startup Fund before it acquired a 23 per cent stake in the company for $1.5 billion in late 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Wiggers |first1=Kyle |last2=Zeff |first2=Maxwell |date=2025-05-21 |title=Jony Ive to lead OpenAI's design work following $6.5B acquisition of his company |url=https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/21/jony-ive-to-lead-openais-design-work-following-6-5b-acquisition-of-his-company/ |access-date=2025-06-03 |website=TechCrunch |language=en-US}}</ref><ref name=":2">{{Cite news |last1=Hammond |first1=George |last2=Criddle |first2=Cristina |last3=Acton |first3=Michael |date=2025-05-21 |title=OpenAI to buy Jony Ive's io for $6.4bn in hardware push |url=https://www.ft.com/content/8ac40343-2fd1-4035-9664-47c77017d0d3 |access-date=2025-06-03 |work=Financial Times}}</ref>

On May 21, 2025, OpenAI announced the full acquisition of io and the merger of both companies.<ref name=":1" /> The deal valued the company at $6.5 billion making it OpenAI’s largest acquisition to date.<ref name=":2" /><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-05-21 |title=Apple iPhone designer Sir Jony Ive joins ChatGPT-maker OpenAI |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5y66yemjdmo |access-date=2025-06-03 |website=BBC News |language=en-GB}}</ref> In an announcement by Sam Altman and Jony Ive, io will merge with OpenAI to "work more intimately with the research, engineering and product teams in San Francisco."<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sam and Jony introduce io |url=https://openai.com/sam-and-jony/ |access-date=2025-06-03 |website=openai.com |language=en-US}}</ref> The merger was announced with a nine-minute film shot in [[Cafe Zoetrope]], San Francisco, in which Altman and Ive discussed their friendship and how they aim to "create a family of devices that would let people use AI to create all sorts of wonderful things."<ref>{{Cite news |last=Milmo |first=Dan |date=2025-05-22 |title=iPhone design guru and OpenAI chief promise an AI device revolution |url=https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/may/22/iphone-design-guru-openai-chief-promise-ai-device-revolution |access-date=2025-06-03 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref><ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Chayka |first=Kyle |date=2025-05-28 |title=Sam Altman and Jony Ive Will Force A.I. Into Your Life |url=https://www.newyorker.com/culture/infinite-scroll/sam-altman-and-jony-ive-will-force-ai-into-your-life |access-date=2025-06-03 |magazine=The New Yorker |language=en-US |issn=0028-792X}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-05-21 |title=iPhone designer Jony Ive joining OpenAI as part of $6.5 billion deal - CBS News |url=https://www.cbsnews.com/news/jony-ive-iphone-designer-apple-openai/ |access-date=2025-06-03 |website=www.cbsnews.com |language=en-US}}</ref> On July 9, 2025, OpenAI and io announced the completion of the merger.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Field |first=Hayden |date=2025-07-09 |title=OpenAI closes its deal to buy Jony Ive’s io and build AI hardware |url=https://www.theverge.com/news/703114/openai-io-jony-ive-sam-altman-ai-hardware |access-date=2025-07-17 |website=The Verge |language=en-US}}</ref>

At the point of acquisition io had 55 employees, all of which joined OpenAI including founders Scott Cannon, Evans Hankey and Tang Tan.<ref name=":3" /><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-05-21 |title=OpenAI recruits legendary iPhone designer Jony Ive to work on AI hardware in $6.5B deal |url=https://apnews.com/article/jony-ive-openai-chatgpt-52c72786e54f0ead8b04d037c30d6754 |access-date=2025-06-03 |website=AP News |language=en}}</ref> Ive and LoveFrom will remain independent and take on creative and design responsibilities at OpenAI.<ref name=":1" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Clarence-Smith |first=Louisa |date=2025-05-21 |title=Sir Jony Ive's tech start-up to merge with OpenAI in $6.5 billion deal |url=https://www.thetimes.com/us/business/article/sir-jony-ives-tech-start-up-to-merge-with-openai-in-65-billion-deal-plmg3qh29 |access-date=2025-06-03 |website=www.thetimes.com |language=en}}</ref>

It was reported that OpenAI and io would release the first of its devices in 2026.<ref name=":3" /> In 2026, ''[[Wired (magazine)|Wired]]'' reported that OpenAI was abandoning the 'io' branding for its hardware due to a trademark lawsuit, and that the hardware itself had been delayed to 2027.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Zeff |first=Maxwell |title=OpenAI Abandons ‘io’ Branding for Its AI Hardware |url=https://www.wired.com/story/openai-drops-io-branding-hardware-devices/ |access-date=2026-02-12 |work=Wired |language=en-US |issn=1059-1028}}</ref>
