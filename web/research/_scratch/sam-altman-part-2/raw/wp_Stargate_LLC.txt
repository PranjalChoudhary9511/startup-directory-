== Background ==
[[File:The White House - American AI investment.webm|thumb|Footage from the press conference released by the [[White House]]<ref>{{Cite tweet |user=WhiteHouse |number=1881851205523271913 |date= January 21, 2025|title= President Trump has unveiled a $500 BILLION American AI investment alongside tech leaders Larry Ellison, Masayoshi Son, and Sam Altman, set to create 100,000 American jobs almost immediately! America is leading the way into the future! 🇺🇸 |access-date= March 10, 2025|link=https://twitter.com/WhiteHouse/status/1881851205523271913 |url-status=live |archive-url= |archive-date= |quote= |ref=}}</ref>]]
The term "Stargate" was used by OpenAI and Microsoft in 2024 to designate an earlier US$100 billion AI [[supercomputer]] project.<ref name=":1">{{Cite news |date=January 21, 2025 |title=Trump Announces Project Stargate, a Massive New Investment in AI |url=https://www.inc.com/ben-sherry/project-stargate-massive-new-trump-era-investment-in-ai/91109890 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250121232408/https://www.inc.com/ben-sherry/project-stargate-massive-new-trump-era-investment-in-ai/91109890 |archive-date=January 21, 2025 |access-date=January 21, 2025 |work=[[Inc.com]]}}</ref> It was named after the 1994 film [[Stargate (film)|''Stargate'']], in which the [[Stargate (device)|stargates]] were portals to other worlds.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Hayden |first=Erik |date=2025-01-22 |title=Project Stargate: Trump Plots With Larry Ellison, Sam Altman On $500B AI Initiative |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/stargate-trump-openai-ellison-1236114430/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250123140326/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/stargate-trump-openai-ellison-1236114430/ |archive-date=January 23, 2025 |access-date=2025-01-24 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Announcement ==
On January 21, 2025, President Donald Trump announced the venture at a [[White House]] press conference, accompanied by [[Sam Altman]] from [[OpenAI]], [[Larry Ellison]] from [[Oracle Corporation|Oracle]] and [[Masayoshi Son]] from [[SoftBank Group|SoftBank]].<ref name=":0" />  Trump called it "the largest AI infrastructure project in history", and he indicated that he would use emergency declarations to expedite the project's development, particularly regarding [[energy infrastructure]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gedeon |first=Joseph |date=January 21, 2025 |title=Trump unveils $500bn joint AI venture between OpenAI, Oracle, and SoftBank |url=https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/jan/21/trump-ai-joint-venture-openai-oracle-softbank |access-date=January 21, 2025 |website=The Guardian}}</ref>  Larry Ellison contended that Stargate could lead to the AI-facilitated production of [[mRNA vaccine]]s against cancer, and that such vaccines could be designed "robotically", or by leveraging AI, "in about 48 hours".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Klee |first=Miles |date=24 January 2025 |title=Anti-Vax Conspiracy Theorists Freak Out Over Trump's $500 Billion AI Plan |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/conspiracy-theory-anti-vax-ai-infrastructure-trump-1235245054/ |access-date=24 January 2025 |website=RollingStone |archive-date=January 24, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250124182919/https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/conspiracy-theory-anti-vax-ai-infrastructure-trump-1235245054/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

According to Ellison, 10 [[data centers]] were being built in [[Abilene, Texas|Abilene]], [[Texas]],<ref name=":1" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Comment |first=Sebastian Moss |date=January 21, 2025 |title=OpenAI announces 'The Stargate Project': $500bn over four years on AI infrastructure |url=https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/openai-announces-the-stargate-project-500bn-over-four-years-on-ai-infrastructure/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250121231902/https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/openai-announces-the-stargate-project-500bn-over-four-years-on-ai-infrastructure/ |archive-date=January 21, 2025 |access-date=January 21, 2025 |website=www.datacenterdynamics.com |language=en}}</ref> with plans to expand to more states and countries, like the United Kingdom, Norway, Japan and the United Arab Emirates.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-02-03 |title=SoftBank and OpenAI seize the moment with new Stargate AI initiative |url=https://www.euronews.com/business/2025/02/03/softbank-and-openai-seize-the-moment-with-new-stargate-ai-initiative |access-date=2025-05-26 |website=euronews |language=en}}</ref><ref name=":3">{{Cite web |last1=Partsinevelos |first1=Kristina |last2=Eudaily |first2=Chris |date=2025-05-16 |title=Nvidia, Cisco and OpenAI are backing the UAE Stargate data center project |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/16/trump-middle-east-openai.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250516220318/https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/16/trump-middle-east-openai.html |archive-date=May 16, 2025 |access-date=2025-05-16 |website=CNBC |language=en-US}}</ref> Trump said that he would use [[executive orders]] to help build the venture's infrastructure. The new venture claims that it will create more than 100,000 jobs in the United States. Altman has said that [[SoftBank Group|SoftBank]] will have the "financial responsibility" of the venture and OpenAI will have the "operational responsibility".<ref name=":1" /> [[Arm Holdings|Arm]], [[Microsoft]], [[Nvidia]], [[Oracle Corporation|Oracle]], and [[OpenAI]] are the key initial technology partners.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Seitz |first=Patrick |date=2025-01-22 |title=Nvidia Stock Rallies As Chipmaker Named To Stargate AI Venture |url=https://www.investors.com/news/technology/nvidia-stock-rallies-stargate-ai-project/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250122193405/https://www.investors.com/news/technology/nvidia-stock-rallies-stargate-ai-project/ |archive-date=January 22, 2025 |access-date=2025-01-23 |website=Investor's Business Daily |language=en-US}}</ref>

Coinciding with the project announcement, [[Microsoft]] and OpenAI signed a new agreement that grants Microsoft a [[right of first refusal]] on future OpenAI cloud computing capacity, while allowing OpenAI to use other cloud providers if Microsoft declines.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Robison |first=Kylie |date=January 21, 2025 |title=Microsoft is letting OpenAI get its own AI compute now |url=https://www.theverge.com/2025/1/21/24349006/microsoft-openai-compute-partnership |website=The Verge |access-date=January 25, 2025 |archive-date=January 31, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250131194919/https://www.theverge.com/2025/1/21/24349006/microsoft-openai-compute-partnership |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Infrastructure development and partnerships==
On April 17, 2025, the Financial Times reported that OpenAI and Oracle weighed a future investment in the [[United Kingdom]] as they explored overseas locations to build out AI infrastructure.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-04-17 |title=OpenAI, SoftBank Weigh U.K. Investment for Stargate AI Venture, FT Says, Citing Sources |url=https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202504175513/openai-softbank-weigh-uk-investment-for-stargate-ai-venture-ft-says-citing-sources |access-date=2025-04-17 |website=Morningstar, Inc. |language=en}}</ref>

On May 16, 2025, Nvidia, [[Cisco]], and OpenAI announced plans to help build the "UAE Stargate" artificial intelligence data center in [[United Arab Emirates]].<ref name=":3" /> UAE Stargate is expected to open in 2026. On May 22, 2025, the UAE AI firm [[G42 (company)|G42]],  [[Oracle Corporation|Oracle]], [[SoftBank Group]] and again [[OpenAI]], Nvidia and Cisco announced a partnership to build Stargate UAE.<ref>{{cite news |last=Subin |first=Samantha |date=22 May 2025 |title=OpenAI, Oracle and NVIDIA will help build Stargate UAE AI campus launching in 2026 |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/22/stargate-uae-openai-nvidia-oracle.html |access-date=July 17, 2025 |work=CNBC |publisher=}}</ref>

On September 23, 2025, OpenAI announced 5 new data center sites under Stargate with sites being located in [[Shackelford County, Texas]]; [[Doña Ana County, New Mexico]]; [[Lordstown, Ohio]]; [[Milam County, Texas]]; and a [[Midwestern United States|midwestern]] United States site whose location is not yet publicly known. This brings Stargate to nearly 7 gigawatts of planned capacity and over $400 billion in investment over the next three years.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Seetharaman |first=Deepa |date=2025-09-24 |title=OpenAI, Oracle, SoftBank plan five new AI data centers for $500 billion Stargate project |url=https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/openai-oracle-softbank-plan-five-new-ai-data-centers-500-billion-stargate-2025-09-23/ |access-date=2025-09-26 |work=Reuters |language=en}}</ref> They said that this puts them on a clear path to securing the full $500 billion, 10-gigawatt commitment they announced in January, by the end of 2025, ahead of schedule.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-09-22 |title=OpenAI, Oracle, and SoftBank expand Stargate with five new AI data center sites |url=https://openai.com/index/five-new-stargate-sites/ |access-date=2025-09-24 |website=OpenAI |language=en-US}}</ref>

On October 10, 2025, OpenAI and [[Sur Energy]] signed a letter of intent to explore the development of an AI data center in [[Patagonia|Argentina's Patagonia]], named Stargate Argentina. This project, the first Stargate site in [[Latin America]], would represent an estimated investment of up to $25 billion and have a capacity of up to 500 megawatts, making it the largest in Latin America, surpassing existing facilities in [[Brazil]], [[Chile]] and [[Mexico]]. If completed, it is expected to accelerate AI development in the region while bringing economic opportunities to a [[Patagonia#Climate|traditionally cold]] and [[List of Argentine provinces by population|sparsely populated region]].<ref>{{Cite news |date=2025-10-10 |title=OpenAI, Sur Energy weigh $25 billion Argentina data center project |url=https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/openai-sur-energy-weigh-25-billion-argentina-data-center-project-2025-10-10/ |work=Reuters}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-10-07 |title=Argentina's AI opportunity |url=https://openai.com/global-affairs/argentinas-ai-opportunity/ |access-date=2025-10-14 |website=OpenAI |language=en-US}}</ref> {{Asof|May 2026}}, there had been no further progress on the project beyond the letter of intent, with Argentina's Economy Ministry stating that they had not received a formal application for their large venture incentive scheme.<ref>[https://www.batimes.com.ar/news/economy/chau-gpt-no-trace-of-mega-billion-dollar-ai-project-announced-before-elections.phtml No trace of mega-billion-dollar AI project announced before elections] - Buenos Aires Times, 28 May 2026</ref>

[[AMD]] will supply up to 6 gigawatts of its [[AMD Instinct|Instinct GPUs]],<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sigalos |first=MacKenzie |date=2025-10-14 |title=OpenAI's latest deals show an aggressive pivot to control every part of its business |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/14/open-ai-hyperscaler-broadcom-chips.html |access-date=2025-11-08 |website=CNBC |language=en}}</ref> and OpenAI may buy a 10% stake in AMD if milestones are met.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sigalos |first=MacKenzie |date=2025-10-06 |title=AMD stock skyrockets 23% as OpenAI looks to take stake in AI chipmaker |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/06/openai-amd-chip-deal-ai.html |access-date=2025-11-09 |website=CNBC |language=en}}</ref> [[Broadcom]] will also supply 10 gigawatts of custom hardware.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bass |first=Dina |last2=Ghaffary |first2=Shirin |date=2025-10-13 |title=OpenAI, Broadcom sign 10-gigawatt pact for chips, networking |url=https://www.afr.com/technology/openai-broadcom-sign-10-gigawatt-pact-for-chips-networking-20251014-p5n29c |access-date=2025-11-08 |website=Australian Financial Review |language=en}}</ref>
