Four writers researched this book in parallel: one on the profile, contact card and photos, and three on the chapters. They worked mainly from Mukesh Ambani's own interviews (Rediff 1998, MoneyLIFE 2007, McKinsey 2025), Reliance filings and speeches, court orders and established press. A separate fact-checker then opened the cited pages again, machine-compared every quotation with the source text, re-checked the numbers, confirmed each contact channel and social link on the publisher's own page, and read photo licences from the Commons API; that pass corrected or removed wrong and weak claims, replaced Wikipedia citations with primary sources or established press, and took out two photos without a free licence. Some pages block automated reading, so these were checked only through a search index or other reports of the same facts: McKinsey, Reuters, Bloomberg, Forbes news, bp and The Economist's headline; the search budget ran out near the end, and we did not read the Simi Garewal interview itself, only a report of it, or the full 2025-26 annual report.
