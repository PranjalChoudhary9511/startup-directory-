Updates Vigilance Development Development Flagship Program Make in India Matters related to MSMEs Skill Development IIPE RGIPT Refinery Capacity The country&#39;s refining capacity has increased from a modest 62 Million Metric Tonnes Per Annum (MMTPA) in 1998 to 248.9 MMTPA at present comprising of 23 refineries - 18 under Public Sector, 3 under Private Sector and 2 in Joint Venture (JV).
