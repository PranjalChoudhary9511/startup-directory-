Rationale and key rating drivers
The rating assigned to the bank facilities of Reliance BP Mobility Limited (RBML) derives significant strength from its strong and
resourceful parent, i.e., Reliance Industries Limited (RIL, rated `CARE AAA; Stable/CARE A1+') holding a majority equity stake of
51%. The rating derives significant strength from RBML's strong operational, financial and managerial linkages including fuel
sourcing arrangements with RIL, which has presence across various segments of the energy value chain. The rating also derives
significant comfort from 49% equity stake in RBML being held by BP Global Investments Limited (BPGIL), which is a subsidiary
of BP Plc, which is also a strong player in oil and gas sector globally and has long-standing partnership with the RIL group in oil
and gas exploration business. The rating also considers comfort from strategic importance of RBML to its promoters, operational
synergies it derives from both the promoter groups and RBML's growing presence in the petroleum fuel retail business with
favourable medium-term growth prospects.
The long-term rating is, however, constrained by the strong competition faced by the company from public sector undertakings
(PSU) oil marketing companies (OMC), which capture major share in the petroleum retail market, the inherent regulatory risks
amidst volatile crude prices as well as crack spreads, low-profit margins due to trading nature of operations, and the risks
associated with partly debt funded capex plans of the company.

Analytical approach: Standalone
Furthermore, the rating also factors strong operational, financial, and managerial linkages with its parent, viz., RIL (holding 51%
stake in RBML). RIL has undertaken to the lender of RBML to maintain its minimum 51% equity stake in RBML and retain
management control during the tenor of bank facilities availed by RBML.

Outlook: Stable
The `Stable' outlook on the long term rating of RBML reflects CARE Ratings' expectation that RBML's strong parentage along with
its linkages with RIL shall result in sustenance of its comfortable financial risk profile and strong liquidity.

Detailed description of the key rating drivers:
Key strengths
Financially strong and resourceful promoters with vast experience in the oil and gas sector
RBML is a joint venture (JV) between two renowned groups in the oil & gas sector, i.e. Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) holding
a controlling stake of 51% and BP Plc (BP) through BPGIL holding 49% stake in the company as on September 30, 2023. RIL has
undertaken to RBML's lender to continue to hold its 51% equity stake in the company during the tenure of sanctioned bank
facilities. RIL is India's largest private sector company, with diverse interests, including oil and gas exploration and production, oil
refining and petrochemicals. RIL has strong competitiveness in the global oil refining and petrochemicals business arising from
its integrated operations. BP, the parent company of BPGIL, is a global energy giant with over 100 years of experience across the
oil and gas value chain and is one of the largest international energy companies with vertically integrated operations including
exploration & production, refining, distribution and marketing, power generation and trading.

Strategic importance of RBML to its promoters
The RIL and BP groups have a long-standing relation underlined by their partnership in the oil & gas exploration and production
business in India as well as gas trading and distribution business in India. To further expand their presence in the energy value
chain, both the groups partnered and formed RBML as a JV to take over the existing fuel retailing business of RIL looking at the
prevalent growth opportunities in this segment. Deriving operational synergies from both of its promoter groups, RBML entered

in gaseous fuel segment with CNG outlets. In line with the emphasis of GoI, RBML is also entering in retailing of Bio-CNG.
Additionally, RBML, leveraging the strengths of its promoters, has ventured into electric vehicles (EV) charging segment by way
of setting up EV fixed charging and battery swapping infrastructure across all Metros, Tier-I and Tier-II cities in phases.
Accordingly, RBML is expected to be strategically important for its promoters in the long-term as both the groups have plans to
reduce their carbon footprints.
The board of directors of RBML comprises three directors from the RIL group, two directors from the BP group and two
independent directors. The management team of RBML has representatives from both of its promoters, with CEO from the RIL
group and CFO from the BP group.

Strong sourcing arrangement with promoters
RBML procures almost its entire fuel requirement from RIL's refineries. Through BP group, RBML's fuel retail outlets have access
to high-quality differentiated fuels, lubricants, on-the-go convenience stores and advanced low carbon mobility solutions.

Growing presence in petroleum retail business with increasing number of outlets
Before the transfer of business to RBML, RIL had around 1400 fuel retail outlets, which later increased to 1,656 outlets as on
September 30, 2023. The company intends to rebrand all the existing fuel outlets under the Jio-bp brand. The company has a
plan to expand its fuel retail outlet network. In addition to fuel retail network, going forward, it has a plan to add EV charging
stations, battery swapping operations, convenience stores at its fuel stations, CNG/Bio-CNG stations along with off-fuel forecourt
quick service restaurants. With increase in the number of outlets across all the above-mentioned formats, RBML is expected to
witness healthy growth in its retail footprint in the medium term.

Favourable growth prospects for RBML's fuel retailing business along with its venture into the gaseous fuels and
EV charging infrastructure
India's energy demand is closely related to its economic growth; therefore, the domestic demand of petroleum products is
expected grow steadily in the medium term supported by the healthy economic growth. This provides healthy growth opportunities
for the fuel retailers like RBML to meet India's steadily growing demand for energy. In addition to this, as the sale of EVs is
gaining momentum, the demand for charging infrastructure has increased. Thus, rapidly growing demand for EV charging
infrastructure in India, rapid proliferation of CNG as alternative fuel along with steady demand for petrol and diesel, is expected
to result in favourable medium-term growth prospects of RBML.

Liquidity: Strong
RBML's liquidity profile is primarily driven by the support of its strong and resourceful parent (i.e. RIL) which has been
demonstrated by the latter through extension of long credit period against purchase of fuels. Furthermore, the company does not
have any debt repayment obligations in the next four year's period. The company also has unutilized fund based working capital
limits to the tune of 375 crore, which provides a liquidity cushion. Also, being a subsidiary of RIL group, it enjoys superior
financial flexibility.

Key weaknesses
Strong competition from PSU oil marketing companies
The domestic fuel retail industry is extremely competitive with public sector undertakings like Indian Oil Corporation Limited,
Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited and Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited having a major share in the fuel retail
segment. The company faces competition from these PSU companies as well as other private players, which are expanding their
existing facilities.

Exposure to volatility in crude oil prices as well as crack spreads and inherent regulatory risk
Oil and petroleum product prices are a function of global demand-supply dynamics which is primarily influenced by Organization
of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC) policies, geo-political situation in countries with oil reserves, economic growth,
exchange rates etc. These factors result in high level of volatility in input costs for fuel retailers like RBML. With the deregulation
of MS prices in June 2010 and HSD prices in September 2014, the OMCs are free to revise the retail prices daily mitigating the
risk of the volatility in crude oil prices to an extent. However, retail prices of MS and HSD do not move exactly in tandem with the
cost, due to intense competition and GoI policy, which limits the pricing freedom.
During FY23, amidst high crude oil prices and consequent losses on the sale of MS and HSD, RBML sales through its retail outlets
witnessed 65% y-o-y decline in its total operating income (TOI) to 14,834 crore along with operating loss (loss before interest,
depreciation, and tax) of 993 crore in FY23. However, with easing of crude oil prices post November-2022 onwards, retail
margins on the sale of MS and HSD improved and the company reported TOI of 16,194 crore and PBILDT of 1,159 crore in
H1FY24.

Inherently low profitability margins associated with the trading nature of operations
The company's profitability margins are inherently low due to trading nature of its business, wherein it procures downstream
petroleum products from refineries and sells it to retail customers/dealers. In case of company operated outlets, its margin
depends on prevailing retail selling price whereas in case of dealer-operated outlets, its margin depends on price at which it sells
to dealers for sales to retail customers at prevailing retail selling price.

Partly debt-funded capex plans
The company has a plan to expand its fuel retail outlet network and refurbish its existing outlets under the Jio-BP brand. The
company has added 196 retail outlets in FY23 & H1FY24 and has rebranded around 80% retail outlets to Jio-BP brand. Initially,
RBML intended to fund the capex through its internal accruals as well as from the available liquidity. However, amidst adverse
business scenario during FY23, the company management decided to partly fund the capex by debt. RBML availed term debt of
500 crore in FY23 and additional 400 crore during H1FY24 to fund its capex. RBML had overall gearing of 1.14 times as on
March 31, 2023. With improved business scenario and cash accruals during H1FY24, the company management intends to further
accelerate growth plans which will continue to be funded by a mix of internal accruals and long-term debt.
