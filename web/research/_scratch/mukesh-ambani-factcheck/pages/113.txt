Nita Ambani has always been in the spotlight for her corporate achievements and role as the matriarch of the Ambani family. In a recent interview, to a leading TV network, she shared a more personal and deeply emotional side, opening up about family, traditions, and the unconditional support that shapes her world.A mother's pride: The wedding that meant so much moreWhen reflecting on the grandeur of her son’s wedding, Nita Ambani took a moment to express her perspective on the criticisms about the celebration. “Every parent wants to do the best for their child,” she explained, addressing the comments that surrounded the event. She saw the wedding as more than just an extravagant affair; it was an opportunity to showcase the rich heritage of India. By bringing Indian traditions and culture to the forefront, Nita emphasized how the celebration was a true reflection of the ‘Make In India’ ethos.However, one specific moment during the wedding left a lasting emotional mark on her. Speaking about her son Anant’s personal struggle with obesity due to asthma, she recalled how he confidently stood on stage, proud of who he had become. "It’s not what I am physically, it’s what my heart is," he said. As a mother, Ambani was deeply moved to see her son take his vows, holding his life partner's hand, and reflecting on the strength and courage he had shown through his journey.A blessing in life and marriageIn her conversation, Nita also opened up about her marriage to Mukesh Ambani, sharing how it was one of the most pivotal moments of her life. “Getting married to Mukesh has been a blessing like no other,” she remarked, highlighting the unconditional support that her husband has shown her. With warmth and gratitude, she acknowledged Mukesh’s role not just as a partner but as her strongest pillar.A mother’s heartfelt reflections on her children’s journeysAs Nita spoke about her three children: Akash, Isha, and Anant, there was a clear sense of pride in her words. She shared how each of them has carved their own path, choosing their respective callings, and how this brought her immense joy as a mother.Her eldest son Akash, with his deep passion for technology, now oversees the operations of Jio, a pivotal force in India’s tech industry. Isha, a dynamic female leader, has been making waves in various leadership roles, while Anant has found his passion for wildlife conservation and is now working towards sustainable green energy ventures. For Nita, seeing her children excel in their chosen fields was nothing short of heartwarming. “This is the time for my children to lead and achieve their aspirations,” she expressed with pride.Despite her highly demanding schedule, she emphasised that being present for her children is her highest priority. She said, “I’ll be there for them, whether it’s babysitting or just being home, so they can soar and achieve their dreams.” Her words reflected the nurturing spirit of a mother who is always ready to put her family first.The power of unconditional love and supportThe interview highlights both Nita Ambani’s professional acumen and the core values that have shaped her. Her selfless dedication to her family, her belief in tradition, and her ability to celebrate both the small and grand moments in life reveal a woman who balances the roles of a mother, wife, and corporate leader with grace and sincerity.
