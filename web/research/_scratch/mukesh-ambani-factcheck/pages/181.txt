(Photo: YouTube/@IndiaAI) Rahul Goreja New Delhi 2 min read Last Updated : Feb 19 2026 | 2:55 PM IST Listen to This Article Reliance Industries Chairman Mukesh Ambani on Thursday announced an investment of ₹10 trillion over the next seven years to build artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure and services in India. "Jio will now connect India with the intelligence era. We will deliver intelligence to every citizen, every sector of the economy and every facet of social development and every service of government," Ambani said during the keynote address at AI Impact Summit in New Delhi. He said the investment, to be made by Jio together with Reliance, will begin this year. “This is not a speculative investment... This is patient discipline, nation building and strategic resilience for decades to come,” he said. Ambani further said the biggest constraint in AI today is scarcity and high cost of compute, and India cannot afford to "rent intelligence". He added that Jio will reduce the cost of intelligence as dramatically as it did the cost of data. Outlining Jio’s plans to reduce this cost, he said the company will build India’s sovereign compute infrastructure through a gigawatt (GW) scale data centre. "Construction has already begun on a multi-gigawatt AI infrastructure in Jamnagar. Over 120 megawatts will come online in the second half of 2026, with a clear path to gigawatt-scale capacity for training," he said. He added that an edge-compute layer, deeply integrated with Jio’s networks, will make intelligence responsive, low-latency and affordable. He also highlighted the group’s green energy capacity and its significance in the AI expansion. "We have an in-house energy advantage with up to 10 GW of ready green-power surplus, anchored by solar in Kutch and Andhra Pradesh," he said.
