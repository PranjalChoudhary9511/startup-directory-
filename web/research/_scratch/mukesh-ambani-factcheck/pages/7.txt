Our multifaceted approach is aimed at creating holistic and sustainable solutions. Education Education is at the heart of Reliance Foundation’s vision for India and central to the country’s social and economic progress. Health We believe that quality and affordable healthcare is crucial not only for communities but also for the economic and social well-being of India. Sports For Development At the heart of our sports initiatives, the goal is to cultivate lifelong learning, build character, and foster leadership skills among India's youth. Disaster Management Reliance Foundation’s Disaster Management programme supports vulnerable hotspots in building safer and disaster-resilient communities. Women Empowerment Empowered women mean a stronger and a better world and we are committed to empowering women to play a catalytic role in bringing change. Arts, Culture & Heritage Reliance Foundation is constantly reimagining ways to protect and promote Indian Arts, Culture & Heritage. Environment & Animal Welfare Creating a better world for all beings through Vantara — a landmark initiative for wildlife rescue, care and conservation, and community-linked sustainability across India Urban Renewal The Urban Renewal initiatives of Reliance Foundation strive to enrich and improve public spaces to improve the quality of life and infrastructure in cities.
