Kamath Rajiv Mehrishi Subsidiaries Allianz Jio Reinsurance Jio Finance Jio Insurance Broking Jio Payment Solutions Jio Leasing Services [ 1 ] Jio Payments Bank (100%) Website www .jfs .in www .jiofinance .in Jio Financial Services Limited ( JFSL ) is an Indian financial services company , based in Mumbai . Originally a subsidiary of Reliance Industries (RIL), it was demerged as an independent entity and listed on the Indian stock exchanges in August 2023. [ 2 ] The company provides financial services, including payment services and insurance broking. [ 3 ] [ 4 ] Its subsidiary Jio Finance holds an NBFC license from the RBI . Another subsidiary, Jio Payments Bank , is also a payments bank registered in India. History [ edit ] Jio Financial Services was initially a financial services subsidiary of Reliance Industries . In July 2023, it was spun-off via a demerger , [ 2 ] with shareholders of Reliance Industries receiving one equity share of Jio Financial Services for every share they held in Reliance. [ 5 ] Reliance Industries transferred ₹ 15,500 crore (US$1.6 billion) of cash and liquid investments to Jio Financial Services as part of the demerger scheme. This gave Jio Financial Services a liquid asset base of ₹ 20,700 crore (US$2.1 billion) . [ 6 ] JFSL was listed on the stock exchanges on 21 August 2023. Following the demerger and listing, JFSL was briefly a part of Nifty 50 , BSE SENSEX and FTSE indices, but was removed from these indices over the following weeks as it did not meet their inclusion criteria. [ 7 ] [ 8 ] [ 9 ] In July 2023, JFSL entered the asset management company (AMC) business, by forming a 50:50 joint venture with BlackRock called Jio BlackRock. [ 10 ] In April 2024, JFSL and BlackRock announced a partnership in wealth management and broking business. [ 11 ] In May 2024, JFSL launched JioFinance, an app with digital payments, loans and insurance offerings. [ 12 ] In March 2026, the reinsurance service “Allianz Jio Reinsurance Limited,” operated jointly with Allianz , began operations. [ 13 ] In August 2026, Jio Financial Services entered into a joint venture agreement with Bank of America, which agreed to invest ₹18,268 crore to acquire up to a 49.9% stake in its subsidiary, Jio Credit Limited. [ 14 ] Controversies [ edit ] Related party transactions [ edit ] In 2024, Jio Financial Services established a subsidiary called Jio Leasing Services Ltd, an unrelated leasing services business.
