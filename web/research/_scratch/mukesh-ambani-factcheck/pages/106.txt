At the 46th annual general meeting (AGM) of shareholders of India’s largest private sector company Reliance Industries (RIL) on August 28, chairman and managing director (CMD) Mukesh Ambani, 66, made an announcement that would be music to all shareholders of the company—that he would continue to be CMD of the oil-to-telecom giant for another five years.Considering that Ambani had donned the CMD’s hat in 2002 upon the death of his father and Reliance Group patriarch Dhirubhai Ambani, he has already been at the helm for 21 years. Another five years would make it 26, after which he plans to step down. Mukesh Ambani would be 71 years old then.At the AGM, Ambani also announced the induction of his three children—twins Akash and Isha, 31, and Anant, 28—into the RIL board subject to shareholder approval. In December 2021, at the Family Day event that coincided with Dhirubhai’s birthday, Mukesh had said that his company will go through “momentous leadership transition”—from seniors belonging to his generation to young leaders. What was at play was a mix of young and experienced blood, with the three young Ambanis assisted in respective roles by senior and experienced professionals.The announcement at the AGM demonstrated one thing clearly—that Mukesh Ambani wants to give his children, especially the older Isha and Akash, more time to spruce up their skills before taking on the mantle from their father in running the Rs 9 lakh crore empire with net profit of Rs 66,702 crore in 2022-23. The reason why shareholders will be relieved by Mukesh’s decision to continue is that RIL is in an expansionary phase, and would benefit from his acumen and experience in handling big projects and deals. From a petrochemical and oil behemoth, it has forayed successfully into consumer-facing sectors like retail and telecom. At the AGM, Ambani announced the launch of the fixed-wireless broadband offering from Jio called Jio AirFiber that will help the company bypass the need for last-mile fibre.Jio AirFiber can handle 150,000 connections per day, which is a 10-fold increase from the earlier figure, and expand Jio’s addressable market over the next three years to over 200 million high-paying homes and premises. RIL will create 2,000 MW of AI (artificial intelligence)-ready computing capacity, across both Cloud and Edge locations. Over the next five years, it plans to shift most of its energy footprint in connectivity and digital services to green energy. Jio Financial Services has just listed on the stock exchanges. RIL is also progressing on its committed capital deployment of Rs 75,000 crore to build a new energy manufacturing ecosystem. This will enable it to accelerate giga-scale production for round-the-clock power and for large-scale green hydrogen production for green chemicals manufacturing, as well as for green mobility.At present, the RIL board, chaired by Mukesh Ambani, has three executive directors—Nikhil R. Meswani, Hital R. Meswani and P.M.S. Prasad. The non-executive independent directors are Arundhati Bhattacharya, K.V. Chowdary, Yasir O. Al-Rumayyan, Adil Zainulbhai, R.S. Gujral, K.V. Kamath and Shumeet Banerji. Mukesh was pulled out of Stanford University, where he was pursuing an MBA. Mukesh ensured that his children went through a similar grind. The grooming of the next generation into leadership roles at group businesses has been going on for around nine years.Akash and Isha joined Reliance Jio Infocomm and Reliance Retail Ventures as directors at the age of 24. Akash is now chairman of Reliance Jio Infocomm, while Isha runs Reliance Retail. Isha, who graduated from Yale University with majors in psychology and South Asian studies in 2013, had a brief stint with McKinsey in the US. Akash, who graduated from Brown University with a major in economics, worked closely on RIL’s 4G telecom venture before becoming a director at the group firms. Both Akash and Isha are said to be key negotiators in mega deals that Jio Platforms, RIL’s digital arm, struck with the likes of Google, Facebook and Intel to turn the group into a futuristic enterprise in 2020. They were also involved in all big acquisitions by Jio Platforms, including Den Networks, Hathway Cable and Datacom.Anant Ambani, 26, is a graduate from Brown University and sits on the board of Reliance New Energy Solar Limited.
