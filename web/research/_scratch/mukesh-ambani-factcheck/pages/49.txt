After listing on the bourses, Jio Platforms shares are holding steady despite the stock market declining over 1 per cent, dragged down by IT companies, rising crude oil prices, and caution ahead of the US Federal Reserve's policy decision. 
Jio Platforms shares made a strong debut on the bourses. The company's market cap stood at Rs 12.50 lakh crore, making it the second-largest by market cap. Reliance Industries is the largest company with a market cap of Rs 16.19 lakh crore. 
After a strong debut on the bourses, Jio Platforms shares gained to hit a high of Rs 1,375, up Rs 17 from the listing price of Rs 1,358.  
Shares of Reliance Industries-backed Jio Platforms made a strong debut on the bourses today, October 28, 2026, listing at Rs 1,358 on the National Stock Exchange (NSE), a premium of 21.36 per cent over the issue price of Rs 1,119. 
In the pre-opening session, Jio Platforms shares settled at Rs 1,358 on the National Stock Exchange (NSE), a 21.36 per cent premium to the issue price of Rs 1,119.

According to information on the BSE and NSE websites, Jio Platforms shares will list on Indian stock exchanges today. A pre-open session for the company's shares will begin at 9:45 am. The opening price will be determined during this time, while trading will begin at 10 am on the BSE and NSE.  
Jio Platforms' IPO is a major milestone for India's capital markets. The issue of Rs 30,213 crore is larger than Hyundai Motors India's Rs 27,870 crore IPO. Thus, Jio has set the record for the country's largest IPO. The company's IPO valuation is estimated at approximately RS 10.3 lakh crore. At this size, Jio could become one of India's most valuable companies upon listing. The company's subscriber base of over 530 million customers reflects its large business network. 
Market experts advise investors who have received share allotments to hold their investments for the long term. They believe that, given Jio's growth potential, investors should not view it solely through the lens of making a profit on listing day. 
Ahead of the listing, the Jio Platforms IPO is commanding a grey market premium (GMP) of around Rs 247 today. With an upper price band of Rs 1,119, the company's shares are expected to list around Rs 1,366, reflecting a grey market premium of 22.07 per cent.

On the BSE, the stock debuted at the same price, and the company's market cap stood at Rs 12,50,586 crore, making it the second most valuable firm by market capitalisation. Earlier, the initial public offering of Jio Platforms closed with bids for 22.19 times the shares on offer, as total demand across the public issue and anchor investor placement crossed Rs 7.4 lakh crore and applications approached a world-beating 1.39 crore. The issue was fully subscribed in just 100 minutes. It comprised an entirely fresh issue of 27 crore shares. The Rs 30,213 crore IPO is larger than Hyundai Motors India's Rs 27,870 crore IPO. Thus, Jio has set the record for the country's largest IPO.
