The lifestyle of India's richest business tycoons often piques public curiosity, and Mukesh Ambani, Chairman of Reliance Industries, is no exception. Recently, his wife Nita Ambani offered a rare glimpse into the billionaire's dietary habits during a visit to the Kashi Vishwanath temple ahead of her son Anant's wedding, revealing a surprisingly disciplined and health-conscious approach.\u00a0 While trying a number of Varanasi snacks at a local shop, including aloo chaat, she disclosed Mukesh's love for the delicacy, indulging in it from time-to-time. "Mukesh would have loved it," she said. However, she also\u00a0shed light on Mukesh Ambani's strict vegetarian diet and eating patterns. Ahead of offering prayers at Kashi Vishwanath temple, she told reporters, "Today I am here with the invitation for the wedding of Anant and Radhika to offer it to the almighty." Kanikka Malhotra, consultant dietician and certified diabetes educator delves into the specifics of Mukesh\u2019s diet, exploring how one of the world's richest men maintains his health through careful food choices and lifestyle habits. Mukesh Ambani's preference for home-cooked meals and their contribution to his overall health and well-being Malhotra states, \u201cA disciplined diet and preference for home-cooked meals offer significant advantages for his health, especially given his demanding schedule.\u201d Home-cooked food allows him to control ingredients, she says, reducing unhealthy fats, sugars, and sodium often found in processed foods. This can lower the risk of chronic diseases. Traditional Indian meals emphasise vegetables, lentils, and whole grains, providing a balanced intake of nutrients for sustained energy and gut health. Portion control at home also helps with weight management. Malhotra adds, \u201cA consistent, familiar diet can help manage stress and reduce decision fatigue, especially for someone with high-pressure responsibilities. Balanced meals provide sustained energy throughout the day, which is vital for focus and productivity during long work hours.\u201d A vegetarian diet eliminates saturated fat from meat, potentially lowering the risk of heart disease. Vegetarian meals are also typically high in fibre, promoting gut health and feelings of satiety, which can be helpful when managing a busy schedule, she states. Certain yoga poses can aid digestion (Source: Freepik) How do yoga and meditation complement one\u2019s dietary habits? According to Nita, Mukesh practises yoga and meditation regularly. These are known to complement one\u2019s dietary habits, working together to optimise his physical and mental well-being. According to Malhotra, this is how it works:\u00a0 Synergy with Diet Meditation cultivates mindfulness, Malhotra stresses, which can translate to mindful eating. This can help him savour his food, avoid overeating, and make conscious choices that align with his healthy diet.\u00a0 Physical Benefits Certain yoga poses can aid digestion, further supporting the benefits of a balanced diet by promoting nutrient absorption and gut health, according to Malhotra. Mental and Emotional Benefits She explains, \u201cBoth yoga and meditation are well-known for their stress-reducing effects. This complements his diet by reducing stress hormones that can negatively impact metabolism and overall health.\u201d Regular yoga and meditation practice can enhance focus and concentration, she continues, which can be crucial for making clear decisions throughout a demanding day. \u201cYoga and meditation can promote better sleep quality, which is essential for physical and mental recovery, especially with a busy schedule.\u201d Advantages of a Gujarati diet Mukesh Ambani's diet emphasises traditional Gujarati meals and avoids junk food entirely. From a nutritional perspective, there are many advantages to such a diet. Malhotra says, \u201cTraditional Gujarati cuisine often features a good balance of vegetables, lentils (dal), and whole grains like rice and roti. This provides a good source of fibre, vitamins, minerals, and complex carbohydrates for sustained energy and gut health.\u201d Avoiding junk food minimises intake of unhealthy fats, processed sugars, and excessive sodium, all linked to chronic diseases like heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and some cancers.\u00a0 Gujarati meals are typically less calorie-dense compared to junk food, making it easier to maintain a healthy weight. However, while balanced, some Gujarati meals might be lower in certain micronutrients like Vitamin B12 (often found in animal products). Consulting a dietician can help ensure he gets all the essential vitamins and minerals.\u00a0 \u201cMaintaining a strict diet during social gatherings can be challenging. He might need to find healthy alternatives or be mindful of portion sizes when eating out. A long-term, entirely vegetarian diet with limited options outside Gujarati cuisine might lead to boredom or difficulty finding suitable meals when travelling,\u201d Malhotra mentions.\u00a0 How Mukesh\u2019s dietary choices inspire or influence broader dietary trends Malhotra reveals, \u201cHis focus on a healthy diet demonstrates that prioritising well-being is crucial for success, even at the highest levels of business. This can inspire others to take their health seriously and make positive dietary changes.\u201d The emphasis on light breakfasts and time-efficient yet nutritious lunches and dinners showcases how healthy eating can fit into a busy schedule. This resonates with individuals in high-pressure roles who often struggle with time management. A balanced and sustainable diet can lead to increased energy levels and focus, potentially influencing others to move away from fad diets or unhealthy eating patterns that may hinder long-term performance.
