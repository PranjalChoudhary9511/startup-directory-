Akash Ambani, son of Reliance Industries Ltd chairman Mukesh Ambani, launched the new iPhone 8 and iPhone 8+ whith Jio specifics offers and the special plans, at the Reliance Corporate Park, on September 29, 2017, in Mumbai, India. - Sipa Press MUMBAI—India’s richest man is catapulting hundreds of millions of poor people straight into the mobile internet age. Mukesh Ambani, head of Reliance Industries, one of India’s largest conglomerates, has shelled out $35 billion of the company’s money to blanket the South Asian nation with its first all-4G network. By offering free calls and data for pennies, the telecom latecomer has upended the industry, setting off a cheap internet tsunami that is opening the market of 1.3 billion people to global tech and retailing titans.
