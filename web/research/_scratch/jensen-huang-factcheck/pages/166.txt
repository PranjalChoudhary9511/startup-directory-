 ITEM 1. CHANGES IN CONTROL OF REGISTRANT 
 &#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160; A change in control of IKOS
Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (&#147;IKOS&#148;) occurred on March 26, 2002.
Pursuant to the terms and conditions of the Agreement and Plan of Merger and
Reorganization, dated March 12, 2002, by and among Fresno Corporation, a
Delaware corporation (&#147;Purchaser&#148;), Mentor Graphics Corporation, an Oregon
corporation (&#147;Mentor&#148;) and IKOS (the &#147;Agreement&#148;) which continued a cash tender
offer for 100 percent of the issued and outstanding common stock of IKOS
(the &#147;Common Stock&#148;) at a purchase price of $11.00 per share in cash
commenced&#160; by Mentor on December 7,2001.&#160; On March 26, 2002 Mentor accepted for
payment 7,836,636 shares of Common Stock, resulting in Mentor&#146;s ownership of
approximately 85 percent of IKOS&#146; Common Stock. Mentor used its existing
cash resources to fund the acquisition of the Common Stock. The total
consideration paid by Mentor for the 7,836,636 shares was $86,202,996. 
 &#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160; In addition, pursuant to Rule
14d-11 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, Mentor and Purchaser
have elected to provide a subsequent offering period for IKOS stockholders who
have not yet tendered, during which all properly tendered shares will be
accepted, and tendering stockholders will receive $11.00 per share following
acceptance.&#160; The subsequent offering
period will expire at 12:00 midnight New York City time on the earlier of (1)
the date upon which shares of IKOS&#146; Common Stock validly tendered and not
withdrawn, together with shares of IKOS&#146; Common Stock then owned by Mentor and
Purchaser, represent at least 90% of IKOS&#146; outstanding Common Stock or (2)
Tuesday, April 23, 2002. 
 &#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160; Upon obtaining 90% of IKOS&#146;
outstanding Common Stock, Mentor intends to complete a short-form merger to
acquire the remaining outstanding IKOS shares.&#160;
The remaining stockholders of IKOS will receive $11.00 per share in cash
upon the closing of the merger.&#160;
Currently the merger is expected to close in April 2002.&#160; If Mentor does not acquire 90% of IKOS&#146;
Common Stock in the subsequent offering period and is therefore unable to
complete a short-form merger, IKOS will hold a special stockholders&#146; meeting to
complete the merger with Mentor in accordance with the terms of the Agreement.
