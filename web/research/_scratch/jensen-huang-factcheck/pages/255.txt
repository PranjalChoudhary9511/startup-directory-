 Jensen Huang’s Net Worth Nears $200 Billion After Nvidia Says It Will Acquire Hugging Face
 Antonio Pequeño IV, Forbes Staff 
 September 4, 2026 2 min read 
 NVDA 
 ORCL 
 Topline 
 Jensen Huang's estimated net worth nearly reached $200 billion Friday, rising alongside Nvidia's stock one day after the company announced it will acquire artificial intelligence model hub Hugging Face. 
 Nvidia announced the Hugging Face acquisition Thursday. Key Facts 
 Huang's estimated net worth rose 1.1% to the $199 billion mark, according to Forbes' Real-Time Billionaires list. 
 Huang's wealth is linked to his roughly 3% stake in Nvidia, which agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion . 
 The jump brought Huang to the No. 8 spot on the real-time list, just behind Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison ($201.2 billion). 
 Nvidia shares traded up a little over 1.5% on Friday, continuing a string of positive performances in the last five days of trading that have raised its stock 6.3%. 
 Big Number 
 24%. That is how much Nvidia's stock has risen since the start of the year, reaching around $231 per share after sitting around $188 per share in January. 
 Key Background 
 Nvidia expects the Hugging Face deal to close in 2027 and has committed to keeping the platform open for developers and models. Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue told CNBC the acquisition talks began this summer, saying open-source AI "needed more resources, more scale, more visibility." He also attributed the recent cyber attack against Hugging Face on engineering mistakes, revealing his company used a Nvidia version of a Chinese open model to resolve it. He said the breach proved Hugging Face needed to "double down" on the growth of open-source AI. Hugging Face was valued at $4.5 billion after a private funding round in 2023 that raised money from backers including Nvidia and Google.
