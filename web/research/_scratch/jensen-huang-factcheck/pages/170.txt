 The Acquisition Playbook .
 Three patterns run through NVIDIA's acquisitions — what it looks for, how it pays, and how it folds in what it buys.
 01
 Acquisition criteria
 Buy software and interconnect that make the GPU more valuable.
 Nvidia targets capabilities that deepen its platform — Mellanox for networking, Cumulus and Run:ai for the software stack, DeepMap for autonomous driving.
 Bright Computing Excelero Run:ai SchedMD (Slurm) MediaQ 
 02
 Capital deployment
 One landmark deal, many tuck-ins.
 Mellanox ($6.9B) is the outlier; most others — Cumulus, Bright Computing, Run:ai, Excelero — are undisclosed capability buys, and the $40B Arm attempt was abandoned.
 Bright Computing Excelero Run:ai SchedMD (Slurm) MediaQ 
 03
 Integration approach
 Fold into the data-center platform.
 Acquisitions are integrated to sell more full-stack accelerated computing, bundling networking, orchestration and software with the GPU.
 Bright Computing Excelero Run:ai SchedMD (Slurm) MediaQ
