 NVIDIA TO ACQUIRE PORTALPLAYER 
 &#151;Combination to Create Application Processors to Power 
 Next Generation of Consumer Mobile Devices&#151; 
 SANTA CLARA, CA&#151;NOVEMBER 6, 2006&#151; NVIDIA Corporation (Nasdaq: NVDA), the worldwide leader in programmable graphics technologies, today announced that it has signed a definitive agreement to acquire San
Jose, CA-based PortalPlayer, Inc. (Nasdaq: PLAY), a leading supplier of semiconductors, firmware, and software for personal media players (PMPs) and secondary display-enabled computers. PortalPlayer, which was founded in 1999, is widely recognized
for its pioneering technology, including the high-performance system-on-chip (SoC) technology which powers some of the world&#146;s most recognizable portable digital music players. 
 Under terms of the agreement, NVIDIA will pay $13.50 in cash for each outstanding share of PortalPlayer common stock, which represents a total purchase price of approximately $357 million, or approximately $161
million net of cash on PortalPlayer&#146;s balance sheet as of September 30, 2006. The purchase price represents approximately a 19 percent premium to the 20-day average closing price of PortalPlayer through Friday, November 3, 2006. This
acquisition has been approved by the Board of Directors of each company and is subject to regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions. 
 &#147;Modern mobile devices are miniaturized yet powerful multimedia computers. At the core of their architectures are complex Application Processors integrating microprocessors, system logic, networking, and multimedia processors,&#148;
said Jen-Hsun Huang, president and CEO of NVIDIA. &#147;With this acquisition, we are combining the two essential technologies of next-generation PMPs, PDAs, portable game players, and phones: PortalPlayer&#146;s innovative Application Processor
technology and NVIDIA&#146;s industry-leading GPU technology. With the products created through this combination, we intend to drive the next digital revolution, where the mobile device becomes our most personal computer.&#148; 
 &#147;We are excited about the next chapter in our corporate history,&#148; added Richard Sanquini, chairman of the
PortalPlayer Board of Directors. &#147;We believe the deal is a win-win for our employees and shareholders. We have spent the past several months extensively exploring our strategic options. We feel the advantages offered by the NVIDIA acquisition
are the most compelling, and that our customers and employees will benefit from NVIDIA&#146;s strong position in handheld GPU technologies and its vast resources and global reach.&#148; 
 The acquisition of PortalPlayer is expected to accelerate NVIDIA&#146;s ongoing investment in its handheld product strategy. Today, NVIDIA&#146;s handheld technology, which combines high-quality graphics, TV, and
video with low power and a small footprint, is at the foundation of many industry-leading portable media devices, including phones from Motorola, Samsung, Kyocera, HTC, and Sony Ericsson. 
 Goldman, Sachs & Co., served as financial advisor to NVIDIA. Cowen and Company, LLC served as financial advisor to PortalPlayer.

 About PortalPlayer 
 PortalPlayer develops semiconductor, firmware and software platforms for portable multimedia products such as personal
media players and personal media display-enabled computers. PortalPlayer products empower consumers to quickly and easily manage, enjoy and have access to multimedia content and other forms of information. PortalPlayer is headquartered in San Jose,
CA, with offices in Kirkland, WA, Taipei, Taiwan and Hyderabad, India. For more information, visit www.portalplayer.com.
