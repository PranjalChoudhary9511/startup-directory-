Did he mean the same trip on which the men nearly starved to death and their clothes rotted on their bodies? Yes, but that was a long time ago, he said. \n&lt;/p&gt;\n&lt;p&gt;\n Eight decades later it would still be a tough hike. That year had brought a lot of snow, so it was a particularly daunting proposition. But since everyone else -- Rocky, Reilly and Danny -- was up for it, there was no way I was going to wimp out. Plus, a younger Scout, a guy named Chip, was game. I had to go. \n&lt;/p&gt;\n&lt;p&gt;\n The plan was to climb the Low Divide pass, descend to the Quinault River, and then hike the same trail back, staying each night in log shelters along the way. We'd make a fire, get warm and eat. \n&lt;/p&gt;\n&lt;p&gt;\n Mike said we'd take a vote: head back or push on to our final destination. He didn't need to spell out that going back meant aborting our mission to reach the river. \n&lt;/p&gt;\n&lt;p&gt;\n \"What do you think, Dan?\" Mike asked.
