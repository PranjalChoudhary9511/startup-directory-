 Bill Gates leaves Microsoft’s board, stepping further away from the tech giant he co-founded
 The executive, one of the world’s wealthiest people, resigned board positions at Microsoft and Berkshire Hathaway to focus more of his time on philanthropic work
 March 13, 2020 More than  6 years ago 
 loading Loading... 
 Bill Gates in November at the New Economy Forum in Beijing. (Takaaki Iwabu/Bloomberg News) 
 By Jay Greene 
 SEATTLE — Bill Gates, who co-founded Microsoft and led the company as it became a technology juggernaut, is stepping down from the company’s board to spend more time on his philanthropic work.
 Gates, who made the announcement Friday afternoon, said in a LinkedIn post that he wants to “dedicate more time to philanthropic priorities including global health and development, education, and my increasing engagement in tackling climate change.” He declined to comment beyond the post, his spokeswoman Bridgitt Arnold said.
