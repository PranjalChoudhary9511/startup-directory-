 Lawsuit against Bill Gates over &#x27;vaccine damage&#x27; continues, judges in Leeuwarden find themselves competent 
 Summary by Algemeen Dagblad 
 The lawsuit against businessman and philanthropist Bill Gates continues. He is accused by a Dutch group of corona sceptics of &#x27;vaccine damage&#x27; by corona vaccines. Gates had objected, because according to him the judges did not have jurisdiction. The court in Leeuwarden ruled on Wednesday that it does have jurisdiction.
