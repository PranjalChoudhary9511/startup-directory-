 Bill Gates confirms he has serious girlfriend, says of marriage to Melinda French Gates that he&#x27;d &#x27;do it again&#x27;
 “We’re having fun, going to the Olympics and lots of great things,” he told the "TODAY" show about his girlfriend, Paula Hurd.

 Feb. 4, 2025, 11:00 AM EST 
 By Minyvonne Burke 
 Bill Gates briefly opened up about why he feels his divorce from Melinda French Gates was his biggest regret , but said he has moved on from the split and is enjoying spending time with his girlfriend, Paula Hurd.
 "My business career, although there's been some ups and downs, has been incredibly phenomenal, my kids are phenomenal, so it's even hard for me to say, you know, complain about things," Gates said on NBC's "TODAY" show when asked about a recent interview with The Times of London .
 During the interview, the Microsoft billionaire had said his divorce was "the mistake I most regret."
 But it seems Gates, 69, is in a much better place.
 "I moved past the divorce," he said on the "TODAY" show. "And Melinda's doing well. I got a lot of work that I love to do so I'm not really complaining about anything."
 He added: "The divorce wasn't great. Having those three kids, the work we were able to do together — even if I knew that it wouldn't last forever, I would still do it again."
 Gates said he has moved on and is "lucky to have a serious girlfriend."

 Gates and French Gates announced in 2021  that they were separating after 27 years of marriage. The following year, Gates was spotted at sporting events with Hurd, a philanthropist who was married to Oracle CEO Mark Hurd until his death in 2019, TODAY.com reported .
 Since then, Gates and Hurd have walked the red carpet together, and attended a Baylor University basketball game and the 2024 Olympics.
 In his memoir, "Source Code: My Beginnings," Gates gave his girlfriend a shoutout.
 "Early readers of the manuscript included Paula Hurd, Marc St. John, and Sheila Gulati. The close read from dear and trusted friends provided much-needed thoughtful and insightful feedback at critical stages in the writing," he wrote, according to TODAY.com.
