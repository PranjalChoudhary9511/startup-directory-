<article class="container post-23177 almanac type-almanac status-publish hentry area_of_achievement-overseas ttd_topic-bill-gates ttd_topic-bill-melinda-gates-foundation ttd_topic-global-health ttd_topic-hiv-aids ttd_topic-infectious-disease ttd_topic-malaria ttd_topic-microsoft-corporation ttd_topic-philanthropy ttd_topic-poliomyelitis ttd_topic-rotavirus ttd_topic-tuberculosis ttd_topic-vaccination ttd_topic-world-health-organization">
	<div class="custom-row">
				<div class="post-content full-width-column ">

						<p>	Bill Gates often explains in interviews how he decided to become a philanthropist: He was &ldquo;exclusively focused&rdquo; on Microsoft in the mid-1990s when his attention was captured by an article about how rotavirus kills half a million children per year by severe diarrhea. That mostly unreported misery seemed cruel and unnecessary, and inspired him to form his foundation in 1994 with an initial stock gift of $94 million. From the beginning his efforts were particularly devoted to improving health in poor countries overseas, because &ldquo;every life has equal value.&rdquo;</p>
<p>	In 2008, Gates left Microsoft and became a full-time philanthropist. From its inception through 2014, his foundation gave away $34 billion, most of it for health-related work. Just in 2014, the $3.9 billion distributed by the Gates Foundation in direct grants included the following major health investments:</p>
<ul>
<li>		Eradicating polio, $442 million</li>
<li>		Agricultural development, $442 million</li>
<li>		Vaccinating people in poor countries, $327 million</li>
<li>		Battling HIV/AIDS, $223 million</li>
<li>		Battling malaria, $201 million</li>
<li>		Battling tuberculosis, $145 million</li>
<li>		Improving maternal and child health, $135 million</li>
<li>		Battling enteric and diarrheal diseases, $100 million</li>
<li>		Battling pneumonia, $100 million</li>
<li>		Battling neglected diseases, $100 million</li>
<li>		Improving sanitation and water hygiene, $96 million
<p>			In a typical year, the Gates Foundation spends about as much as the World Health Organization on global health. It has been estimated that Gates directly saved 8 million lives in its first two decades, and headed off untold human misery, via its attacks on infectious diseases.</p>
</li>
</ul>
