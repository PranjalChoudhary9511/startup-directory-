 From the Editor
 Last Updated Mar 10, 2026, 1:01am EDT
 Zhang Yiming is the main cofounder of Chinese tech giant ByteDance, best known for its insanely popular app TikTok, which has more than 1 billion users worldwide. 
 It also has interests in e-commerce, education, gaming and news.
 Zhang stepped down as chairman of ByteDance in 2021 after resigning as CEO earlier that year, reportedly under pressure from the Chinese government. 
 In 2026, the company ceded control of TikTok's U.S. business to a consortium of mostly American investors including Oracle and Silver Lake Management, which allowed the service to keep operating on U.S. soil. 
 In China, the tech giant is making strides in AI. Its Doubao AI assistant counted 345 million monthly active users and became the country's most popular AI-powered app in the first quarter of 2026, according to intelligence service provider QuestMobile.
