NEW YORK, NY - MAY 21: Google co-founder and CEO Larry Page speaks during a news conference at the Google offices on May 21, 2012 in New York City. (Photo by Justin Sullivan/Getty Images) Key Facts Page's estimated net worth was $234.4 billion at around 12 p.m. EDT on Friday, down $1.5 billion for the day as Alphabet shares dipped 0.7%, according to Forbes' Real-Time Billionaires list. Dell's estimated worth stood at $229.2 billion, down $17.5 billion for the day as Dell shares fell over 10% on Intel's disappointing earnings and broader AI spending fears that are hitting the entire tech sector for a second straight day. Page, Google's cofounder, lost $31 billion on Thursday when Alphabet's stock fell 12% after the company posted third-quarter revenue figures that fell short of investor expectations. Dell shares are down after Intel, in reporting its quarterly earnings on Thursday, warned that "we see the PC slowdown continuing at least through the first half of next year"—a worrying statement for Dell, which is still reliant on PCs for a large portion of its revenue even as the company shifts towards AI infrastructure. KEY BACKGROUND Intel shares fell 29% on Friday after the company forecasted revenue between $15 billion and $16 billion for the current quarter, falling below Wall Street's estimates. In its earnings statement, the company also said its making "significant, long-term and inherently risky investments" in research, development and manufacturing facilities, worrying investors at a time when return on investment for substantial AI spending is a major concern. Intel expects that PC shipments will fall 12% this year and another 10% next year—a highly concerning statement for Dell, which makes PCs, laptops and servers. Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan cited supply shortages and rising memory prices as reason for this expected drop, adding he expects the drop to last at least through the first part of 2027. FORBES VALUATION As the tech sector enters the second day of the latest tech selloff, the top eight richest people have all lost some of their worth today, according to Forbes' Real-Time Billionaires list. Heading into Friday afternoon, Elon Musk was down $30 billion, bringing his estimated net worth to $886.8 billion, Jeff Bezos was down $2.7 billion with a $361.6 billion net worth and Sergey Brin sat at $216.5 billion, down $709.4 million for the day. Mark Zuckerberg lost $670 million, bringing his estimated worth to $213.9 billion, while Jensen Huang lost $6.5 billion, bringing his net worth to $178.7 billion, and Larry Ellison lost $8.8 billion to stand at $172.5 billion.
