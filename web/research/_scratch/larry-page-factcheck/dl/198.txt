Responding to question from Charlie Rose in an onstage interview at a TED conference in Vancouver, Mr. Page suggested businesses can be better vehicles to create “revolutionary change” and build a better future. He praised Mr. Musk, the founder of car company Tesla and spacecraft designer SpaceX, for aspiring to mount a mission to Mars to “to back up humanity,” which Mr. Page called “a worthy goal.” “That’s a company, and that’s philanthropical,” he said. He added, “We have a lot of employees at Google who’ve become pretty wealthy.
