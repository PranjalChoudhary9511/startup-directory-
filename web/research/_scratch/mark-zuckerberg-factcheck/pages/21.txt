Explore Roles Candidate Journey Better Together Internship Program Alumni Network Search Read Our Blog Search Grants The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative makes grants* to organizations working in support of our work. Grants are awarded via various funding entities. In science, our grantmaking is aligned with our four scientific grand challenges. Since our launch in 2015, CZI has committed over $7.22 billion in grants. This database of our grant giving is retroactive to 2018 and is updated twice a year. See additional information below on the database terms and disclosure timeline. To learn more about our grantees, visit the CZI Newsroom . *Grants and grantmaking refer to our various charitable funding types, including grants, gifts, awards, charitable sponsorships, and program-related investments (PRIs).
