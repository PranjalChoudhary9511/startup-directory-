Explore Roles Candidate Journey Better Together Internship Program Alumni Network Search Read Our Blog Search About CZI The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative was founded in 2015 by Priscilla Chan and Mark Zuckerberg to help solve some of society’s toughest challenges — from curing or preventing disease to improving education and addressing the needs of our local communities. We provide the operational support across our areas of work. Our Leadership Photo by Cody Pickens for Forbes Photo by Cody Pickens for Forbes Our Focus Areas Science We’re leading the new era of AI-powered biology. At the Biohub, we bring together scientists, engineers, and AI experts with a shared mission to help scientists cure or prevent all disease. Learn More (Opens in a new window) Education Our mission is to bring learning science into the tools used every day by teachers and students, ensuring that technology reflects the realities of classrooms and strengthens teaching and learning. Learn More (Opens in a new window) Supporting Our Work Grants & Ventures Through grantmaking and our venture portfolio, we invest in bold ideas that have the potential to accelerate change. Since 2015, CZI has awarded approximately $7.22 billion in grants and invested nearly $300 million in ventures whose work aligns with our values. Learn More Local Giving Supporting Our Communities Mark and Priscilla support the communities where they live and work by partnering with local nonprofits, supporting local solutions, and addressing emerging needs to help these communities thrive. Learn More CZI carries out its work through the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, LLC; the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Foundation (a 501(c)(3) private foundation); the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) at the Silicon Valley Community Foundation; and Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Advocacy, (a 501(c)(4) organization).
