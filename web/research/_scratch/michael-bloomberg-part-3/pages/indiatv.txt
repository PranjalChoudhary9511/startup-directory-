 PM Modi and Michael Bloomberg announce partnership to advance Smart Cities Initiative
 India TV News Desk 
 Published: Feb 16, 2015, 08:07 PM IST ,Updated: Feb 16, 2015, 09:59 PM IST

 pm modi and michael bloomberg announce partnership to advance smart cities initiative 
 Image Source : INDIATV 
 New Delhi: Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and former Mayor of New York City Michael Bloomberg, today announced a partnership between Bloomberg Philanthropies and the Ministry of Urban Development to advance the government's ambitious smart cities initiative. 
 Under the partnership, Bloomberg Philanthropies will provide funds and other assistance to Urban Development Ministry to implement the Smart Cities project in select cities.
 The announcement was made after Bloomberg's meeting with Modi.
 Smart Cities Initiative promotes economic growth, improve governance, and deliver more effective and efficient public services to India's urban residents.
 “This approach is different from the conventional approach, which involved preparation of Detailed Project Reports, and their appraisal and approval by the Central Government,” a government release said.
 "Bloomberg Philanthropies will provide assistance to the Ministry of Urban Development to select cities for Smart Cities Mission funding on a continuous basis," the release mentioned.
 "It will ensure that real citizen engagement happens, as people get involved both in design and execution of city development plans. This will actualise the idea of cooperative and competitive federalism."
 Earlier, Bloomberg met Urban Development Minister M Venkaiah Naidu and discussed various urban development issues including the Smart City project.
 The Prime Minister has described the Smart Cities Initiative as a challenging task, which nevertheless has to be undertaken to improve the quality of life for India's urban citizens with stakeholder's participation.
