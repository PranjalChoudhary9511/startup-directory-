 Incorporated on March 2 by conversion of Adani Exports partnership firm, promoted by Gautam S. Adani and Rajesh S. Adani. Engaged in exporting frozen foods, dyes, intermediates, plastic products, agricultural products to 28 countries. 
 
 1994 1994 
 
 Amalgamated Adani Management Consultancy Services Pvt. Ltd., issued 500,000 equity shares. Public issue of 1,250,000 equity shares; allotted additional 11,900 for oversubscription. 
 
 1995 1995 
 
 Allotted 3,750,000 shares to promoters, friends, relatives; 1,261,900 to public. Amalgamated AMCPL with AEL. 
 
 1996 1996 
 
 Allotted 5,511,800 equity shares in 1:1 ratio; reissued 100 forfeited shares. 
 
 1997 1997 
 
 Reorganised authorised capital; issued preference shares via private placement. Approved issuance of securities up to Rs. 30 crores. Signed MoU with Eastern Generation for coal-fired power projects. Entered JV with Gujarat government for Mundra mega port. Signed letter of intent for Mundra port and 3000-hectare industrial park. Received LOI for 54MW Naptha power plant at Anjar. Incorporated Adani Global Limited in Mauritius as wholly owned subsidiary. 
 
 1998 1998 
 
 Incorporated Adani Eastern Generation Company Ltd as 50:50 JV with Eastern Generation. Promoted Rs. 340 crore Mundra port project on BOOT basis with 30-year concession. 
 
 1999 1999 
 
 Signed MoU with Wilmar Trading Pte for 50:50 JV Adani Wilmar ($6 million equity). Mundra port JV with Gujarat Port Infrastructure Development Corporation operational since October 1998. Declared 1:1 bonus issue. Received GCC Export Appreciation Award for 1997-98. 
 
 2000 2000 
 
 Launched 'Fortune' branded packaged edible oil in Jaipur. Received Texprocil Silver Trophy for fabrics exports (merchant, 1998-99). Received Texprocil Silver Trophy for non-quota fabrics exports (1998-99). Entered agreements with NSDL and CDSL for demat facility. Indravadan A. Modi resigned as Director. 
 
 2001 2001 
 
 Adani Wilmar planned launch of 'Raag' vanaspati brand. 
 
 2002 2002 
 
 Appointed Biswajit Choudhury as Nominee Director. 
 
 2003 2003 
 
 Shanxi Corporation tied up with Adani Exports for 1-1.5 million tonnes coal supply to India. Appointed Birva C Patil as Compliance Officer and Company Secretary. 
 
 2006 2006 
 
 Company changed name from Adani Exports Ltd. to Adani Enterprises Ltd. 
 
 2007 2007 
 
 Appointed Parthiv Parikh as Assistant Company Secretary and Compliance Officer. 
 
 2008 2008 
 
 Appointed Yoshihiro Miwa as Additional Director. Adani Enterprises signed exclusive JV agreement with Chemoil. 
 
 2009 2009 
 
 Adani entered cement business. Adani Enterprises issued 1:1 bonus shares. 
 
 2010 2010 
 
 Adani Welspun Exploration commenced drilling of first well in Gujarat. Adani secured coal purchase rights and take-or-pay arrangement for Indonesia port and rail project. Adani won bid to develop and mine coal from Chendipada Coal Block in Orissa. Adani Enterprises issued rights in 1:16 ratio. 
 
 2011 2011 
 
 Adani Power, subsidiary, received National Energy Conservation Award 2011. Adani Enterprises selected as Business Superbrand. Adani Enterprises partnered with Kowa Company. 
 
 2012 2012 
 
 Adani Group commissioned India's largest solar power plant in Gujarat. 
 
 2013 2013 
 
 Adani began integrated coal MDO operations in India with access to over 3 billion tonnes of coal reserves. 
 
 2014 2014 
 
 Adani and Posco agreed to build rail line in Australia. 
 
 2015 2015 
 
 Adani signed MoU with Woodside Energy for energy cooperation. 
 
 2016 2016 
 
 Adani Wilmar and Ruchi Soya proposed joint venture for FMCG company. 
 
 2017 2017 
 
 Adani Enterprises acquired Adani Infrastructure Pvt Ltd. Adani reached royalties agreement with State Government. Adani project received green light. Adani Australia project faced protests. Adani Group planned container port in Malaysia for global expansion. 
 
 2018 2018 
 
 Adani Defence & Aerospace and Elbit Systems inaugurated India's first private UAV manufacturing facility. 
 
 2019 2019 
 
 Adani Enterprises incorporated JV with Sainik Mining and Allied Services for mining in Chhattisgarh. Adani partnered with Digital Realty to build data infrastructure. 
 
 2020 2020 
 
 Adani Enterprises commenced commercial operations at Lucknow International Airport. 
 
 2021 2021 
 
 Adani Enterprises won India's largest expressway project. Adani Enterprises won Rs. 1838 crore highway project from NHAI in Kerala. 
 
 2022 2022 
 
 AdaniConneX opened flagship data center campus in Chennai. Adani Enterprises achieved financial closure for India's largest greenfield expressway project. Adani Enterprises and Israel Innovation Authority signed MoU for tech solutions. Adani Group achieved financial closure for Navi Mumbai International Airport project. 
 
 2023 2023 
 
 Adani acquired majority stake in IANS India Pvt Ltd. Mundra Solar Energy Limited commissioned manufacturing plant. Adani Enterprises acquired 100% stake in Swarna Tollway Private Limited and 56.8% stake in Gujarat Road and Infrastructure Company Limited. 
 
 2024 2024 
 
 Adani ConneX established USD 144 billion construction financing framework. Adani Defence & Aerospace and EDGE Group signed cooperation agreement in defence and security. Adani unveiled 3000-crore ammunition complex in Uttar Pradesh. 
 
 2025 2025 
 
 Adani Enterprises' Kutch Copper and Caravel Minerals signed strategic MoU for Australian copper project. Adani Defence & Aerospace and DRDO unveiled India's vehicle-mounted counter-drone system at Aero India 2025. Adani Airports secured USD 750 million global financing. Adani Enterprises partnered with MetTube for copper tubes business. Adani Defence & Aerospace partnered with Prime Aero to expand aviation MRO footprint, acquiring 100% stake in Indamer Technics. Adani Enterprises received LoA for Sonprayag-Kedarnath ropeway project.
