 Jeet Adani 
 Director, Airports 
 
 
 Mr Jeet Adani is Director at Adani Airport Holdings Ltd (AAHL), India’s largest private airport infrastructure company, with eight airports in its management and development portfolio. In addition to the airports business, he leads several future-focused verticals across the Group, including artificial intelligence (AI), data centres, defence, petrochemicals and copper, reflecting his expanding role in shaping the Adani Group. 
 
 He joined the Group in 2019 after attending the University of Pennsylvania School of Engineering and Applied Sciences, US. He began his career in the Group Chief Financial Officer’s office, working across capital markets, risk and governance policy, and strategic finance, with exposure to all listed businesses. This early experience provided him with a strong grounding in the Group’s financial architecture and strategic priorities. 
 
 He now leads AI integration across the Adani portfolio and is actively driving partnerships to bring advanced capabilities to India, including a recent $15 billion collaboration with Google. His focus is on building the foundational infrastructure for intelligent systems, spanning affordable green energy, reliable power and large-scale digital connectivity. 
 
 Beyond business, he is deeply engaged in social impact initiatives. Inspired by Dr. Priti Adani’s leadership in expanding the Adani Foundation, the social welfare and development arm of the Adani Group, he actively supports partnerships focused on improving access and opportunities for people with disabilities.
