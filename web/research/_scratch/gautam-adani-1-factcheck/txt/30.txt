The couple got married in a private wedding ceremony today, February 7, which was held in Ahmedabad. Sharing the news, Gautam Adani posted a few pictures of the newly-wed couple Jeet and Diva on social media. His post read (translated into English), "By God's grace, Jeet and Diva got married today. Their wedding was held in Ahmedabad in the presence of close friends and family. It was a small and intimate wedding, and because of this we couldn't invite many of our well-wishers and I would like to apologies for it. I would like you all to please bless our daughter Diva and Jeet." In the wedding pictures, the groom Jeet is seen wearing a light golden coloured sherwani. While the bride, Diva, is seen wearing a beautiful light golden and maroon coloured lehenga. As per reports, Jeet Adani is known to support specially-abled people. And so, he had got Manish Malhotra to collaborate with the NGO Family of Disabled (FOD) to design custom-made shawls for the couple. The NGO also created beautiful hand-painted items like glassware, plates and other accessories for the wedding festivities. Gautam Adani's son Jeet Adani marries Diva Shah: Wedding video Their intimate wedding was not only beautiful celebration of love, but also wedding with a cause. Many artisans from across the country had created elements for the wedding festivities that added a touch of culture to their celebrations. Reports suggests, artisans from Nashik, Maharashtra, crafted exquisite Paithani sarees for the wedding guests; Nitaben from Mundra, Gujarat, created intricate mud work murals; and traditional bangles from Bibaji Choori Wala in Jodhpur brought a splash of colour and heritage to the festivities. Jeet Adani and Diva Shah: The newly-wed couple Jeet Adani is Gautam and Dr Priti Adani's younger son. He is a graduate from the University of Pennsylvania's School of Engineering and Applied Sciences and is also a trained pilot. Jeet Adani is the Director at Adani Airports. Meanwhile, Diva Shah is the daughter of Jaimin Shah, a diamond trader. Diva and her family prefer to lead a private life, as not much is known about them. Gautam and Priti Adani also have an elder son named Karan Adani, who the Managing Director of Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone. He is married to Paridhi Adani and the couple have a daughter together. Jeet Adani and Diva Shah got engaged in 2023 and now, over a year later they are married. Here's wishing Jeet and Diva many years of happiness and togetherness! Get the latest lifestyle news and trends.
