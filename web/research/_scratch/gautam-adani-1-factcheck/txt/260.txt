 Observation and exclusion of companies 
 Until November 2025, the Executive Board of Norges Bank decided whether companies should be excluded from the fund's investment universe or placed on an observation list, in accordance with guidelines established by the Ministry of Finance. 

 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 The Executive Board's decisions on exclusion or observation of companies were based on recommendations from the Council on Ethics, which is appointed by the Ministry of Finance. For the product-based coal criterion, recommendations came from Norges Bank Investment Management. 
 The Government has appointed a committee to review the ethical framework. The committee is to deliver its report by 15 October 2026. Pending a new framework, the Ministry of Finance has established temporary ethical guidelines. 
 Under the temporary ethical guidelines, Norges Bank shall not decide on observation or exclusion, but may revoke previous decisions on observation and exclusion. The Council on Ethics shall continue to monitor the fund's investments and inform Norges Bank about companies identified for possible exercise of ownership. 
 The following alphabetical overview shows the companies which Norges Bank, as at November 2025, had decided to exclude or place under observation. As decisions on exclusion and observation may be revoked under the interim guidelines, the list is updated regularly. The list was last updated on 11 August 2026. In this update, the following company names were removed from the list: Evergreen Marine Corp Taiwan Ltd, Korea Line Corp, Pan Ocean Co Ltd and Bombardier Inc. During the first half of 2026, Norges Bank revoked the exclusion of the first two companies and ended the observation of the last two. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 The Government has appointed a committee to review and deliver a report on the ethical framework by 15 October 2026.

The Government has appointed a committee to review the ethical framework. The committee is to deliver its report by 15 October 2026. Pending a new framework, the Ministry of Finance has established temporary ethical guidelines.
Under the temporary ethical guidelines, Norges Bank shall not decide on observation or exclusion, but may revoke previous decisions on observation and exclusion. The Council on Ethics shall continue to monitor the fund's investments and inform Norges Bank about companies identified for possible exercise of ownership.
The following alphabetical overview shows the companies which Norges Bank, as at November 2025, had decided to exclude or place under observation. As decisions on exclusion and observation may be revoked under the interim guidelines, the list is updated regularly. The list was last updated on 11 August 2026.  In this update, the following company names were removed from the list: Evergreen Marine Corp Taiwan Ltd, Korea Line Corp, Pan Ocean Co Ltd and Bombardier Inc. During the first half of 2026, Norges Bank revoked the exclusion of the first two companies and ended the observation of the last two.
