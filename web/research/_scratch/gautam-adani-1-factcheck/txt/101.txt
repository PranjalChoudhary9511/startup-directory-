 Timeline 
 
 
 
 
 2025 
 Adani Energy Solutions raised Rs 8,773 crore to fund the transmission and smart metering opportunity. It is the largest fundraise in the Indian power sector through a QIP 
 In line with the ESG commitment, AESL has divested 500 MW of Adani Dahanu Thermal Power Plant in Adani Electricity 
 Won Rs 25,000 cr Bhadla-Fatehpur HVDC project, the largest transmission order win till date for AESL 
 The company is first in the Indian power sector to join the Utilities for Net Zero Alliance (UNEZA), a global alliance for clean energy and renewable infrastructure development 
 The Mumbai utility was recognized with a Gold Award at Brandon Hall HR Excellence Awards 2024 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 2024 
 Commissioned largest inter regional 765 KV Warora Kurnool Transmission Line 
 Won bid to install 41.2 lakh smart meters for Andhra Pradesh Discoms 
 Won bid to install 116 lakh smart meters for MESDCL Discom 
 Global Sustainability Leadership Award 2023 from the World Sustainability Congress for sustainable practices and performance 
 Golden Peacock Environment Management Award 2023 from Institute of Directors (IOD) for its commitment to sustainability 

 
 
 
 
 
 2023 
 Received award of Letter of Intent (LoI) for Khavda II A Transmission Ltd 
 Received award of Letter of Intent (LoI) for WRSR Transmission Ltd 
 Won bid to install 7.7 Lakh “Smart Meters” for Assam Power Distribution Company Limited (APDCL) 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 2022 
 Foray into Smart Meter Business by winning Bombay Electric Supply & Transport (BEST) project to install 10.8 Lakh Smart Meters 
 Adani Transmission announced to become net zero by 2050 
 Completed Rs 3,850 Crore Primary Equity Transaction With IHC 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 2021 
 Acquisition of Warora Kurnool Transmission Limited from Essel Infraprojects Ltd 
 Commissioning of Fatehgarh Bhadla Transmission Limited 
 Award of LoI for MP Power Transmission Package II Limited 
 Commissioning of Ghatampur Transmission Limited 
 Award of LoI for Khavda Bhuj Transmission Limited 
 Award of LoI for Karur Transmission Limited 
 Acquisition of MUL 
 
 
 
 
 
 2020 
 Acquisition of Alipurduar Transmission Limited from KPTL 
 Acquisition of KVTPL (Kharghar Vikhroli Project) 
 
 
 
 
 
 2019 
 Award of LOI for KVTPL (Kharghar Vikhroli Transmission Private Limited) 
 Award of LOI for LBTL (Lakadia Banaskantha Transco Limited) and JKTL (Jam Khambaliya Transco Limited) 
 Award of LOI for BKTL (Bikaner - Khetri Transmission Limited) and WTL (WRSS XXI (A) Transco Limited) 
 Commissioning of PPP 8, 9 & 10 project 
 Commissioning of STL, RRWTL project 
 Acquisition of KEC asset 
 
 
 
 
 
 2018 
 Award of FBTL, GTL, OCBTL project 
 Acquisition of Reliance Infrastructure Limited's Power Generation, Transmission & Distribution Business in Mumbai. 
 Commissioning of ATRL Project 
 
 
 
 
 
 2017 
 Award of Public- Private Partnership (PPP) 8, 9 and 10 projects 
 Acquisition of Reliance Infrastructure Limited's assets (WTGL & WTPL) 
 
 
 
 
 
 2016 
 Award of ATRL project 
 Award of NKTL project 
 Acquisition of GMR assets (MTSCL and ATSCL) 
 Completion of 400kV Mundra- Zerda transmission line as an EPC contract 
 
 
 
 
 2015 
 Demerger of Adani Transmission Limited (ATL) from Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) 
 Listing of ATL on BSE and NSE Stock Exchanges 
 Award of STL, RRWTL and CWRTL project 
 
 
 
 
 2014 
 Commissioning of First 765 kV transmission line (Tiroda- Aurangabad transmission line) 
 
 
 
 
 2013 
 Conversion of Mundra system into ISTS as a license company 
 
 
 
 
 2012 
 Commissioning of 400 kV Tiroda-Warora transmission line 
 Commissioning of First HVDC transmission line (&plusmn; 500 kV MundraMohindergarh transmission line) 
 Completion of 400kV Kawai - Chhabra transmission line as an EPC contract 
 
 
 
 
 2011 
 Commissioning of 400kV Mohindergarh-Bhiwani transmission line 
 Commissioning of 400kV Mohindergarh-Dhanoda transmission line 
 
 
 
 
 2010 
 Commissioning of FSC at Sami S/S to support evacuation system 
 
 
 
 
 2009 
 Commissioning of First 400 kV transmission line (Mundra-Dehgam transmission line) 
 
 
 
 
 2006 
 Development of 220kV transmission line for Mundra Thermal Power Station
