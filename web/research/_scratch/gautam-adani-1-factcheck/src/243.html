Two acquitted in Adani kidnapping case

TNN / Updated: Dec 1, 2018, 03:07 IST
Share
AA
Text Size
  • Small
  • Medium
  • Large
Representative image
AHMEDABAD: A sessions court at the Mirzapur rural court campus on Friday acquitted two men who were arrested and tried for kidnapping industrialist Gautam Adani for ransom twenty years ago.
During the trial, when evidence against the Fazl-ur-Rehman and his accomplice Bhogilal Darji was being recorded, Adani did not appear before the court to depose and insisted that his earlier statement, made during the trial against other accused person, ought to be taken into consideration. After this, the prosecution dropped Adani’s name from the list of witnesses.
After the trial, the court did not find adequate evidence against Rehaman and Darji, and acquitted both of them. The court had earlier acquitted half a dozen other accused persons in this case.
The alleged kidnapping had taken place on the evening of January 1, 1998, when Adani and Shantilal Patel were driving from Karnavati Club to Mohammedpura village. The car was intercepted and both of them were kidnapped at gunpoint. They were blindfolded, put in another car and were taken to an unknown place where they were confined in a room. The kidnappers then demanded a ransom.
Then police sub-inspector R K Patel became a complainant in this case. In June 1999, one Mohammed Javed Ansari was arrested in this case. Nine persons, including Bhogilal Darji and Jayesh Darji, were listed as absconders. One decade later, police got custody of Rehman and filed a chargesheet against him.
