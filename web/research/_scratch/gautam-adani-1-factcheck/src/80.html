Redefining the Staples
Food Industry

AWL Agri Business Ltd

Formerly known as Adani Wilmar Limited

We are one of India’s largest Food & FMCG companies, offering a diverse portfolio of essential kitchen staples, including edible oils, wheat flour, rice, pulses, and sugar. Our flagship brand, ‘Fortune’, commands trust of more than 135 million households, reaching to every 1 in 3 Indian families. With nearly 100 manufacturing units, including India’s largest integrated food complex at Gohana in Haryana and largest port-based refinery at Mundra in Gujarat, AWL ensures seamless production and distribution. Our extensive supply chain, supported by 118 stock points, over 10,000 distributors and sub-distributors, along with a retail network of 2.6 million outlets, guarantees widespread accessibility across urban and rural India. Not just in India, our products reach to more than 50 countries with the promise of quality and trust.

Beyond serving retail consumers, we also cater the HoReCa and Institutional sectors, with staple food products, specialized Bakery and Lauric fats, Castor Oil derivatives, Oleochemicals, and value-added Soya products. Our advanced castor oil facilities produce pharmaceutical-grade, and low-moisture castor oil, catering to global markets. Additionally, AWL has expanded into the Home & Personal Care (HPC) segment, offering soaps, handwashes, and multipurpose cleaners, further strengthening our product offerings.

MD & CEO's

Message

Since its inception in 1999, AWL Agri Business Ltd. has built a strong foundation and carved a distinguished place in India's edible oil and food landscape. What began as a vision to deliver purity and quality to every Indian kitchen has today become a movement that touches millions of lives.

From the very start, we recognized the changing aspirations of Indian consumers — driven by rising incomes, evolving lifestyles, and growing health consciousness. Anticipating that India's edible oil demand would far exceed domestic production, we strategically expanded our manufacturing footprint across key port locations. Today, as one of the country's leading importers and refiners of edible oils, we take pride in how our foresight has shaped our leadership.

Over the years, our relentless focus on quality, innovation, and brand building has transformed the edible oil market — moving consumers from loose to hygienically packed oils, enhancing both safety and trust. Our flagship brand Fortune has become India's No. 1 edible oil brand, trusted by more than 131 million households and reaching one in every three Indian families. Fortune has now evolved into a symbol of purity, taste, and reliability — extending its promise to new categories like Basmati Rice, Pulses, Besan, and more.

Alongside Fortune, our Kohinoor brand continues its rich legacy as one of India's most cherished names in premium Basmati rice. Together, these iconic brands embody our unwavering commitment to delivering quality, authenticity, and nourishment to every table.

For today's health-conscious consumers, we have introduced value-added products such as Fortune Rice Bran Oil and the Xpert Range, reflecting our constant drive to innovate for better living.

Beyond India, our footprint extends across borders. In Bangladesh, our edible oil brand Rupchanda leads the market with two state-of-the-art refineries, while our products reach over 50 countries worldwide, carrying forward our promise of excellence. Our strong presence in Castor Oil, Oleo Chemicals, and Specialty Fats further diversifies our contribution to global markets.

As we look ahead, our purpose remains clear — to contribute to a Healthy, Growing Nation. We believe that true progress goes beyond business growth; it is about creating value for communities and empowering lives. Through our CSR initiatives such as Project Fortune SuPoshan, which focuses on improving nutrition and health outcomes among women and children in rural India, we are working to make a lasting social impact.

As I take on the responsibility of leading AWL Agri Business Ltd., I do so with immense pride and a deep sense of purpose. The coming decade will be one of transformation — as we evolve from being a leading edible oil company to a comprehensive food enterprise, committed to serving every Indian household with trust, innovation, and care.

With the passion of our people, the trust of our consumers, and the strength of our iconic brands, I am confident that we will continue to move from strength to strength — nourishing lives, empowering communities, and building a healthier, growing India.

— Shrikant Kanhere

Managing Director & CEO, AWL Agri Business Limited

Integrated Business Model

At AWL Agri Business, we are reimagining the staple foods industry with a unique, multi-category, integrated business model that spans the entire value chain—from farm to consumer.

Our approach ensures that every meal prepared in your kitchen reflects our commitment to quality, trust, and sustainability. Whether you’re a home cook or a culinary professional, our diverse range of high-quality kitchen essentials, including edible oils, rice, pulses, wheat flour, and more, form the heart of one in every three kitchens nationwide.
