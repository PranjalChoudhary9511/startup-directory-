Adani Enterprises Ltd announces H1 and Q2 FY27 results

Reports highest-ever half-yearly EBITDA of Rs. 11,958 cr with 56% growth YoY

ANIL Solar module capacity expansion to 10 GW complete

Data Center business signs up new hyperscale order of 240 MW

Editor’s Synopsis

Consolidated financial highlights H1 FY27 (YoY)

  • Revenue up by 62% to Rs. 71,569 cr
  • EBITDA up by 56% to Rs. 11,958 cr
  • Continuing PBT up by 45% to Rs. 3,297 cr*
  • EBITDA and PBT boosted by capacity ramp-up at Copper, with EBITDA of Rs.2,033 cr
  • AEL receives long-term credit rating upgrade to AA / Stable from CARE, highest rating in its credit history

Consolidated financial highlights Q2 FY27 (YoY)

  • Revenue up by 74% to Rs. 38,023 cr
  • EBITDA up by 62% to Rs. 6,316 cr
  • Continuing PBT up by 146% to Rs. 2,002 cr

Business highlights

  • ANIL Solar module line expanded to 10 GW by commissioning 4.3 GW TopCon in September 2026
  • AdaniConnex signs new hyperscale order of 240 GW for Navi Mumbai data center
  • Ramp up in Copper plant continues with 5.2x increase in sales volume of H1 FY27

*excluding one-time OFAC settlement of Rs. 2,644 cr during Q1 FY27

Ahmedabad, October 28, 2026: Adani Enterprises Ltd (AEL), the flagship company of Adani Group, announced its results today for the second quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026.

The Strong EBITDA growth in the first half of FY27 reflects maturity of investments made over the past several years, with multiple businesses reaching scale and translating capital deployment into tangible earnings growth. This performance underscores the strength of our diversified business portfolio and its growth potential. Backed by strong operating fundamentals, increasing asset maturity and the ramp-up of newly commissioned ANIL module line capacity, AEL is well positioned to sustain this growth trajectory further.

“We live in an age of such intense disruption that the scale of our ambitions must continuously grow,” said Mr Gautam Adani, Chairman of the Adani Group. “What Adani Enterprises has become very effective at is turning increasingly large and complex commitments into execution at scale across our businesses. Our investments in integrated energy, airports and AI ecosystems are recent examples. Perhaps the strongest validation of our execution capabilities is the trust domestic and global investors continue to place in us, choosing to partner with us even amid extreme volatility. As the tailwinds propelling India forward continue to strengthen, we believe we are better positioned than ever to capture the opportunities ahead. We remain bullish on India’s future and, therefore, confident in our own growth story.”

About Adani Enterprises Ltd

Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) is the flagship company of Adani Group, one of India’s largest business organisations. Over the years, Adani Enterprises has focused on building emerging infrastructure businesses, contributing to nation-building and divesting them into separate listed entities. Having successfully built sizeable and scalable businesses like Adani Ports & SEZ, Adani Energy Solutions, Adani Power, Adani Green Energy, Adani Total Gas and Adani Wilmar, the company has contributed to make India self-reliant with our robust businesses. This has also led to significant returns to our shareholders for three decades.

The next generation of its strategic business investments are centered around new energy ecosystem, airport management, data center, roads and primary industries like copper and petrochem - all of which have significant scope for value unlocking.
