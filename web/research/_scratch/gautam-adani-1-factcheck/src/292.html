India's 100 Richest 2026

The net worth of more than half the listees on the 2026 Forbes list of India’s richest decreased in a year of volatile economic environment. Challenges from AI-led automation faced by the software services sector also had an impact on the country’s tech titans. Adani Group Chairman Gautam Adani, though, returned to the No 1 position with a $20.7 billion boost that took his fortune, which he shares with his family, to $112.7 billion. Reliance Industries Chairman Mukesh Ambani is next with $86.3 billion. The minimum net worth to qualify for the list dropped to $3 billion from $3.2 billion last year.
