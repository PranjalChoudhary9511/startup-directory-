About us

Mahendra Brothers, established in 1960, traces its roots back to 1940, when Sri Bagmalbhai Parikh founded the Parikh family’s natural diamond business with sheer passion, courage, and determination. A true pioneer, he became known as one of the architects of the diamond industry.

CSR

Established in 1985, the Parikh Foundation is the CSR initiative of the Parikh Family. What began in Palanpur has grown into a nationwide presence across India. Guided by compassion, ethical responsibility, and respect for all living beings, the Foundation advances inclusive and sustainable development across diverse communities. Its work strengthens access to quality education, advances community health and well-being, promotes environmental conservation, and supports social finance initiatives that empower underserved populations. Through long-term partnerships, strong governance, and a focus on measurable impact, the Foundation continues to build resilient communities and contribute to a more equitable and sustainable future for generations to come.
