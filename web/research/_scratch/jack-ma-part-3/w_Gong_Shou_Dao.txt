'''''Gong Shou Dao''''' ({{zh|c=功守道}}), also known as '''On That Night... While We Dream''' is a 2017 Chinese [[kung fu film|kung fu]] [[short film]] directed by [[Wen Zhang]] and produced by [[Jet Li]]. The film stars [[Jack Ma]], Jet Li, [[Donnie Yen]], [[Wu Jing (actor)|Wu Jing]], [[Tony Jaa]], [[Jacky Heung]], [[Asashōryū Akinori]], [[Zou Shiming]], and [[Natasha Liu Bordizzo]]. The film was released on November 11, 2017, during the Double 11 Shopping Carnival.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://news.ifeng.com/a/20171101/52887642_0.shtml |script-title=zh:马云主演《功守道》：只有20分钟 双十一晚会放精华版 |newspaper=ifeng |date=2017-11-01 |language=zh |archive-date=2017-11-13 |access-date=2017-11-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171113014604/http://news.ifeng.com/a/20171101/52887642_0.shtml |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Plot==
One day, Master Ma is walking down the street, when suddenly he sees the words "[[Huashan Sect]]" ({{zh|s=华山派|p=|labels=no}}) on a sign. He closes his eyes and imagines himself duelling different martial arts masters.

==Production==
Wen Zhang was hired as the director.<ref>{{cite news |author=DRAGON LIN |url=https://asianmoviepulse.com/2017/11/10-facts-jack-ma-jet-lis-gsd/ |title=10 facts about Jack Ma and Jet Li’s ‘GSD’ |newspaper=asianmoviepulse |date=2017-11-04 |archive-date=2017-11-10 |access-date=2017-11-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171110115212/https://asianmoviepulse.com/2017/11/10-facts-jack-ma-jet-lis-gsd/ |url-status=live }}</ref> [[Yuen Woo-ping]], [[Sammo Hung]] and [[Tony Ching]] were hired as choreographers. The film is Chinese e-commerce tycoon Jack Ma's debut.<ref>{{cite news|title=Alibaba's Jack Ma to star in short kung fu film|url=http://www.chinadaily.com.cn/china/2017-11/01/content_33974250.htm|work=Chinadaily|date=2017-11-01|archive-date=2017-11-05|access-date=2017-11-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171105184219/http://www.chinadaily.com.cn/china/2017-11/01/content_33974250.htm|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Music==
The theme song ''Feng Qing Yang'' ({{zh|s=《风清扬》|p=|labels=no}}) is sung by singer [[Faye Wong]] and Jack Ma. The song's title ''Feng Qing Yang'' is the name of a top martial artist in another one of [[Louis Cha]]'s [[wuxia]] novels, ''[[The Smiling, Proud Wanderer]]''.

==Release==
On October 28, 2017, Jack Ma released a poster in his Sina Weibo.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://ent.ifeng.com/a/20171029/42998211_0.shtml |script-title=zh:《功守道》武术指导三人组：地球最高水平 |newspaper=ifeng |date=2017-10-29 |language=zh |archive-date=2018-01-09 |access-date=2017-11-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180109074220/http://ent.ifeng.com/a/20171029/42998211_0.shtml |url-status=live }}</ref> The movie was released in China on November 11, 2017, and was watched 170 million times online in a couple months, according to Alibaba.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.irishtimes.com/business/technology/alibaba-s-jack-ma-to-star-in-his-own-martial-arts-movie-1.3394693|title= Alibaba’s Jack Ma to star in his own martial arts movie|publisher= The Irish Times|access-date= 6 March 2018|archive-date= 9 November 2020|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20201109023733/https://www.irishtimes.com/business/technology/alibaba-s-jack-ma-to-star-in-his-own-martial-arts-movie-1.3394693|url-status= live}}</ref>
