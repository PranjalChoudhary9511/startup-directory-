The '''Jack Ma Foundation''' ('''JMF''') is the [[philanthropic]] foundation of Chinese businessman [[Jack Ma]].<ref name="jdoebele">{{cite web |date=December 2, 2019 |title=Jack Ma Outlines Bold Vision For His Philanthropy Foundation |url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/jdoebele/2019/12/02/jack-ma-shares-his-plans-for-education-philanthropy-in-china/ |work=Forbes}}</ref> It was established in 2014.

==History==
In September 2018, Ma announced that he would retire from the company he founded and the source of his wealth, internet merchandiser [[Alibaba Group|Alibaba]], in order to pursue educational work, philanthropy, and environmental causes.<ref name=dtmm>{{cite news | url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/technology/2018/09/07/alibaba-chief-jack-ma-stand/ | title=China's richest man Jack Ma to stand down from Alibaba | newspaper=The Telegraph | publisher=Daily Telegraph | date=8 September 2018 | accessdate=8 September 2018| last1=Murphy | first1=Margi }}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.latimes.com/business/la-fi-jack-ma-alibaba-20180907-story.html | title=Billionaire Jack Ma prepares for life after Alibaba. He'll retire Monday, report says | publisher=Los Angeles Times | work=LULU YILUN CHEN and TOM MACKENZIE | date=7 September 2018 | accessdate=8 September 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite news | url=https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/business/alibabas-jack-ma-chinas-richest-man-to-retire-from-company-he-co-founded/articleshow/65727702.cms | title=Alibaba's Jack Ma, China's richest man, to retire from company he co-founded | newspaper=[[The Economic Times]] | date=8 September 2018 | accessdate=8 September 2018 | archive-date=25 December 2018 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181225092239/https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/business/alibabas-jack-ma-chinas-richest-man-to-retire-from-company-he-co-founded/articleshow/65727702.cms%20 | url-status=dead }}</ref>

In 2019, the JMF started the Netpreneur Initiative that grants 10 prizes of $1 million each to 10 African entrepreneurs per year<ref name=interview>{{cite news |last1=Cué |first1=Elena |title=Interview with Jack Ma |url=http://www.alejandradeargos.com/index.php/en/all-articles/21-guests-with-art/41731-interview-with-jack-ma |work=Alejandra de Argos |date=12 June 2019 |language=en-gb}}</ref> and launched a fund with $14.6 million to develop education in [[Tibet]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Jack Ma Foundation to support education in Tibet - Chinadaily.com.cn |url=http://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201906/26/WS5d137afaa3103dbf1432a732.html |work=[[China Daily]] |date=26 June 2019}}</ref>

In 2020, the JMF was announced as one of the alliance partners of [[Prince William, Duke of Cambridge|Prince William]]'s [[Earthshot prize|Earthshot Prize]] to find solutions to environmental issues.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Frangoul|first=Anmar|date=2020-10-08|title=Prince William launches £50 million Earthshot Prize, with a nod to John F. Kennedy|url=https://www.cnbc.com/2020/10/08/prince-william-launches-50-million-earthshot-prize.html|access-date=2020-10-13|website=CNBC|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|author=The Editorial Board|date=2020-10-11|title=Opinion {{!}} Save the Planet, Win a Prize|language=en-US|work=The New York Times|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2020/10/11/opinion/prince-william-earthshot-prize.html|access-date=2020-10-23|issn=0362-4331}}</ref>

In January 2025, the JMF marked the 10th anniversary of the Rural Teachers Initiative, with Ma addressing a group of educators as a former teacher.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-01-09 |title=AI to reshape the world, Alibaba founder Jack Ma tells Chinese rural teachers |url=https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3294054/ai-set-reshape-world-alibaba-founder-jack-ma-tells-chinese-rural-teachers |access-date=2025-06-04 |website=South China Morning Post |language=en}}</ref> He also accepted an honorary professorship from the [[University of Hong Kong]]'s business school in November 2024.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2023-04-21 |title=Alibaba founder Jack Ma accepts university teaching post in Hong Kong |url=https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/east-asia/alibaba-founder-jack-ma-accepts-university-teaching-post-in-hong-kong |access-date=2025-06-04 |work=The Straits Times |language=en |issn=0585-3923}}</ref>

===Coronavirus===
In response to the [[COVID-19 pandemic|coronavirus pandemic]], the Jack Ma Foundation announced its intention to donate a total of 500,000 testing kits and one million masks to the [[United States]], stating "we join hands with Americans in these difficult times."<ref>[https://twitter.com/foundation_ma/status/1238317660871393280 Jack Ma Foundation on Twitter: "Through a donation of 500,000 testing kits and 1 million masks, we join hands with Americans in these difficult times.… https://t.co/zltD14xrGW...<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref> The Foundation, along with the [[Alibaba Group]], also donated similar materials to [[Iran]], [[Italy]], [[Spain]], [[Belgium]], [[Japan]], and [[South Korea]].<ref>[https://edition.cnn.com/2020/03/14/tech/jack-ma-face-masks-us-donation/index.html Jack Ma says he will donate one million face masks and 500,000 coronavirus testing kits to the US - CNN<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.politico.com/amp/news/2020/03/13/jack-ma-coronavirus-test-kits-128522|title = Jack Ma to donate 500,000 test kits and 1 million mask to the U.S.| website=[[Politico]] | date=13 March 2020 }}</ref> Ma also announced a donation of 1.1 million tests, six million masks, and 60,000 protective suits to all African countries to combat the pandemic.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.cnn.com/2020/03/16/africa/jack-ma-donate-masks-coronavirus-africa/index.html|title=Chinese billionaire Jack Ma donates masks, test kits to all countries in Africa|first=Anita |last=Patrick|website=CNN|date=16 March 2020 |access-date=2020-03-16}}</ref> For instance, in [[Nigeria]], senior health official Abdulaziz Abdullahi said that the country had received 100,000 face masks, 1,000 protective gowns, and 20,000 test kits.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.news24.com/Africa/News/billionaire-jack-ma-donates-conoravirus-test-kits-to-nigeria-20200326|title = Billionaire Jack Ma donates conoravirus test kits to Nigeria}}</ref>

In March 2020, the JMF published ''Handbook of COVID-19 Prevention and Treatment'', a detailed 68-page book edited by Tingbo Liang that documents the clinical experience obtained at the First Affiliated Hospital of the [[Zhejiang University School of Medicine]] in the fight against the COVID-19 pandemic.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/india/jack-ma-donates-emergency-supplies-to-all-barring-india-twitter-asks-what-indian-billionaires-doing|title=Jack Ma donates emergency supplies to all barring India, Twitter asks what Indian billionaires doing|website=National Herald|date=21 March 2020|language=en|access-date=2020-04-12}}</ref>

While Ma is a member of the [[Chinese Communist Party]], some analysts nevertheless believe that the [[Government of China|Chinese government]] may view Ma's philanthropic efforts unfavorably.<ref>{{cite news |title=Jack Ma: The billionaire trying to stop coronavirus (and fix China's reputation) |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-china-52325269 |accessdate=26 April 2020 |work=BBC News |date=25 April 2020 |language=en-gb}}</ref>
