# Curriculum Map: 8 Pillars, ~405 Tracks

**Numbering:** `P1.03.02` = Pillar 1 · Module 03 · Track 02. On the site, show it as **01.02** inside the pillar, like fanout.
**Legend:** 🆓 = free module (the first module of every pillar is free) · 🎬 = full production (you on camera + motion graphics) · 🖥️ = screen-recorded tool walkthrough (cheap to re-shoot) · 📦 = module deliverable that goes into the Founder Dossier.

**Rule for every track:** one core idea, one deep real story, one "do this now" task. If a track needs two stories, it's two tracks.

**Before filming:** every company fact in this map must be checked against a primary source. Titles here are drafts.
