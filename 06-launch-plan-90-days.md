# 90-Day Launch Plan: from an empty folder to a live product with paying users

**Goal by Day 90:** website live · Pillar 1 (45 tracks) complete · first module of Pillars 2–7 free · 10 Real Stories · Grants directory · 300+ Founding Members · 10,000+ followers across Instagram/YouTube/LinkedIn.

*(These are ambitious but realistic targets for a focused founder with a 3–4 person team. Adjust after Week 4's data.)*

---

## Phase 1: Validate & set up (Weeks 1–3)

### Week 1: Apply Pillar 1 to yourself
- [ ] Pick a working name; buy the domain; claim Instagram/YouTube/LinkedIn/X handles; run a basic IP India trademark search.
- [ ] **Interview 20 people:** 10 college students (Arjun), 10 working professionals (Neha). Ask: what have you tried to learn about starting up? What did you pay for? Why did you stop? What would make you pay ₹3,000?
- [ ] Write your own Problem Brief for this startup.
- [ ] Start the build-in-public series: "Day 1: I'm building the founder school India never had."

### Week 2: Offer + landing page
- [ ] Landing page: headline, trailer placeholder, pillar list, Founding Member offer (₹4,999 lifetime, 1,000 seats), waitlist.
- [ ] Razorpay account + KYC; a payment page for Founding Member.
- [ ] Set up a WhatsApp community for waitlisters.
- [ ] Book a studio day; buy or rent the kit.

### Week 3: Quality bar
- [ ] Script and film **P1.01.01** (sample script in 03-track-production-playbook.md) + 3 Real Story reels.
- [ ] Hire or contract: an editor, a motion designer (per track), a researcher-writer.
- [ ] Publish the 3 reels. Measure retention and saves.
- [ ] **Decision gate:** if 20 interviews + reel response show pull, proceed. If not, rework positioning before spending more.

## Phase 2: Build & presell (Weeks 4–8)

### Weeks 4–5
- [ ] Scaffold the website (Next.js + Supabase + Razorpay + VdoCipher/Bunny); see 04-website-spec.md.
- [ ] Load the curriculum map as seed data.
- [ ] Script Pillar 1, Modules 1–2 (14 tracks). Film day #1.
- [ ] 1 reel per day; 1 long-form YouTube founder story per week.
- [ ] **Presale push:** share P1.01.01 free → CTA for Founding Member.

### Weeks 6–7
- [ ] Track pages, notes, progress, gating live on a preview URL.
- [ ] Grants directory seeded and verified (top 25 schemes).
- [ ] Books page (30 books). Real Stories (first 5 long-form pages).
- [ ] Script Pillar 1, Modules 3–5 (20 tracks). Film day #2.
- [ ] Reach out to 15 college E-Cells: offer a free "Problem to First ₹" workshop.

### Week 8
- [ ] Beta: 50 founding members get early access; collect feedback weekly.
- [ ] Run 2–3 college workshops (online or in person).
- [ ] Script Pillar 1, Modules 6–7 (11 tracks) + Module 1 of Pillars 2–4. Film day #3.

## Phase 3: Launch & learn (Weeks 9–13)

### Week 9–10
- [ ] Film Module 1 of Pillars 5–7. Edit backlog.
- [ ] Finish 10 Real Stories pages.
- [ ] Launch-week plan: countdown reels, a live session, early-bird deadline for Founding Member.

### Week 11: 🚀 Public launch
- [ ] Open the doors: Pillar 1 complete + free first modules of all pillars + grants + books + stories.
- [ ] Launch-day live stream + press outreach (YourStory, Inc42, Entrackr, college media).
- [ ] First weekend challenge (free for members, with a sponsored prize if you have a sponsor; otherwise you fund a modest prize).

### Week 12–13
- [ ] Start Meta ads **only if** organic free→paid ≥ 2%: retarget video viewers and free signups first, at ₹1,000–3,000/day.
- [ ] Weekly release rhythm begins: 4–6 new tracks every week (Pillar 2 next).
- [ ] Review the metrics: signups, activation, conversion, W4 retention, deliverables submitted.
- [ ] Set the next 90 days' plan from the data.
