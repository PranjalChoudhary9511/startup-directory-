# The World's Best Business Storytelling: Reference Library

**Built:** 27 Oct 2026 · **Rule:** views, subscribers and likes were *ignored*. A video is here only for storytelling craft, depth of research, first-hand detail or knowledge you can't get elsewhere. Many of the best picks have a few thousand views.

**How links were checked:** every YouTube link was confirmed live through YouTube's own records (title + channel match) on 27 Oct 2026, and a random sample of 18 was re-checked independently (17 confirmed, 1 flagged below). Anything not confirmed says **(unverified)**.

> ⚠️ **Use rule:** these are *research and craft references*. Their footage, audio and scripts are copyrighted: don't cut them into paid tracks. Learn the story, verify the facts from primary sources, then tell it in your own voice. To use someone's material, license it or collaborate (Think School, Shivanshu, Navan).

**Tags:** 🎬 produced documentary · 🎙️ long-form podcast/interview · 🧑‍🏫 first-hand founder talk · 🗄️ primary-source oral history · ⚠️ caution (re-upload, faceless channel, or fact-check needed)

## Part 1. The storytellers, and what to steal from each

### Tier 1: world-class benchmarks

| Storyteller | Format | What makes it world-class | What to steal for your tracks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Sequoia: *Crucible Moments*](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOhHNjZItNnMOLXiaew-7KxBOA_dEHCX7)** 🎬 | 20–30 min docs (made with Vox Creative) | Only the people who were there: founders, early staff, board. Opens Sequoia's archive (the original YouTube pitch deck). | One turning point per episode, told by witnesses. Your "Real Stories" format. |
| **[Acquired](https://www.youtube.com/@AcquiredFM)** 🎙️ | 3–6 hr podcast | Chronological "seasons" built from S-1s, biographies and insiders, then strategy analysis (Playbook, Power, bull/bear). | Story first, framework second. Matches your Problem Iceberg. |
| **[FRONTLINE PBS](https://www.youtube.com/frontline)** 🎬 | Feature investigations | Dozens of original interviews plus documents, built in three acts. | The structure for failure and "what went wrong" tracks. |
| **[Asianometry](https://www.youtube.com/@Asianometry)** 🎬 | 15–35 min narrated docs | One Taiwan-based researcher working from conference papers and company histories. Deepest engineering literacy on YouTube. | **Right length, right depth.** Plain visuals, so your production can beat it. |
| **[Computer History Museum](https://www.youtube.com/user/ComputerHistory)** 🗄️ | 1–5 hr oral histories | Interviewed by historians, not journalists. Raw first-hand detail, near-zero views. | **Mine these for "wow" details nobody else uses.** |
| **[Founders (David Senra)](https://www.youtube.com/@founderspodcast1)** 🎙️ | One biography per episode | Best passages from founder biographies, linked across centuries. | A "founder psychology" voice; one obsession per story. |
| **[Business Breakdowns (Colossus)](https://www.youtube.com/@joincolossus)** 🎙️ | ~60 min, one investor | Unit economics, moat and history, no filler. ⚠️ Look-alike channels exist; real titles say "[Business Breakdowns, EP. N]". | How the business actually makes money. Your B2B/B2C depth layer. |
| **[60 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/@60minutes)** 🎬 | 13–25 min segments | Access nobody else gets (inside Hermès, inside Nvidia). | Access as a hook. |
| **[Bloomberg Originals](https://www.youtube.com/@business)** 🎬 | Long-form docs + *Hello World* (Ashlee Vance) | Reporter access, archive work. *Hello World* is a host-led field journey, 22–47 min. | Host-on-location format for future field tracks. |
| **[Veritasium](https://www.youtube.com/@veritasium)** 🎬 | Engineering one-offs | The ASML film is the best example of "visual detailing" there is. | How to make an invisible process visible. |
| **[Y Combinator](https://www.youtube.com/@ycombinator)** + **[CS183B lectures](https://www.youtube.com/@ycrootaccess)** 🧑‍🏫 | Talks, interviews | Founders talking to founders, at the moment it happened. | Raw "early days" quotes. |
| **[Stanford eCorner](https://www.youtube.com/@StanfordeCorner)** + **[Stanford GSB](https://www.youtube.com/@stanfordgsb)** 🧑‍🏫 | Talks | A 20-year archive: founders recorded *before* they were famous (Instagram at six months old). | "Before they were famous" reversals. |
| **[Dwarkesh Patel](https://www.youtube.com/@DwarkeshPatel)** 🎙️ | Long interviews | Weeks of prep, adversarial technical questions. The Sarah Paine series is history taught as strategy. | Question craft for your own founder interviews. |

## Part 3. By industry: B2C, B2B, B2G

### The 10 best channels for "how a whole industry works"
1. **[Perun](https://www.youtube.com/@PerunAU)**: an Australian defence-industry consultant. Hour-long, rigorous defence economics. **The best B2G channel on YouTube.**
2. **Acquired**: novel-level company histories.
3. **Business Breakdowns**: the widest industry coverage (Rolex, McKinsey, Markel, Ryanair and more).
4. **Asianometry**: semiconductors and Asian industrial history.
5. **Modern MBA**: 30–50 min industry unit economics (pizzerias, airlines, hotels). Closest to "how a whole industry works".
6. **Wendover Productions**: shipping, airlines, logistics.
7. **[Odd Lots (Bloomberg Podcasts)](https://www.youtube.com/@BloombergPodcasts)**: obscure industry plumbing, explained by practitioners.
8. **[Lenny's Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/@LennysPodcast)**: B2B go-to-market and product craft.
9. **PolyMatter**: industry structure and regulation.
10. **Patrick Boyle**: a skeptical, numbers-first counterweight to hype.
